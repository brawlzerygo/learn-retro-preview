Goat Format Tier List: The Best Yu-Gi-Oh! Decks of 2005

November 23, 2026

Goat format has had the same card pool since 2005, but which decks are on top has still changed over the years. goatformat.com published a tier list in June 2020 that ranks the format's decks from A to E. Here it is, with what the top decks do and links to full lists.

Tier A

  • Chaos Turbo (Thunder Dragon Chaos): Thunder Dragon, flip monsters and discard Traps build a lead, then Chaos Sorcerer and Black Luster Soldier take over. goatformat.com named it the most played deck in October 2026, and Format Library describes today's Goat metagame as centered on it.
  • Chaos Control: Goat Control's Scapegoat and Metamorphosis shell with Chaos monsters on top. Angel Chaos Control, fed by Shining Angel, is ranked here too. See also Thunder Dragon Chaos Control.
  • Goat Control: the deck the format is named after. Scapegoat tokens, Thousand-Eyes Restrict and a long, careful game.
  • Dimension Fusion Turbo and Flip Control round out tier A.

Tier B

Reasoning Gate, Library FTK, Empty Jar, Beatdown, PACMAN and Burn. Four of them win in unusual ways: Reasoning Gate and Library FTK with one big turn, Empty Jar by decking you out, and Burn with effect damage.

Tier C

Soul Control, Heavy Slump Chaos, Chaos Recruiter, Spell Canceller Warrior, Spirits and Gravekeeper's. On this site, the Warrior and Chaos Warrior lists are rated C, next to Spell Canceller Warrior.

Tier D and E

Tier D is Zombies, Skill Drain Burn and Beastdown. Tier E is Rescue Cat, Ben Kei, Cyber-Stein and Exodia.

What the tiers don't tell you

A tier list ranks decks against the whole field. It doesn't say which deck is best against yours. For that, see our Goat matchup chart: every top deck at a glance, how to beat it, and who is favored in each matchup.
