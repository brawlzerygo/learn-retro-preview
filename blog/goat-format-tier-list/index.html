Goat Format Tier List: The Best Decks in Goat

November 23, 2026 · Draft

Goat format has had the same card pool since 2005, but players keep finding better decks. The best-known ranking is goatformat.com's tier list from June 2020, built from what was winning at the time. Here it is, with what the top decks do and links to full lists.

Tier A

  • Chaos Turbo (Thunder Dragon Chaos): Thunder Dragon, flip monsters and discard Traps build a lead, then Chaos Sorcerer and Black Luster Soldier take over. In October 2026 goatformat.com named it the most played deck.
  • Chaos Control: Goat Control's Scapegoat and Metamorphosis shell with Chaos monsters on top. Angel Chaos Control, fed by Shining Angel, is ranked here too. See also Thunder Dragon Chaos Control.
  • Goat Control: the deck the format is named after. Scapegoat tokens, Thousand-Eyes Restrict and a long, careful game.
  • Dimension Fusion Turbo and Flip Control round out tier A.

Tier B

Reasoning Gate, Library FTK, Empty Jar, Beatdown, PACMAN and Burn. Most of these win in unusual ways: a one-turn kill, decking you out, or burning your Life Points to 0.

Tier C

Soul Control, Heavy Slump Chaos, Chaos Recruiter, Spell Canceller Warrior, Spirits and Gravekeeper's. The site's Warrior and Chaos Warrior lists sit here.

Tier D and E

Tier D is Zombies, Skill Drain Burn and Beastdown. Tier E is Rescue Cat, Ben Kei, Cyber-Stein and Exodia: fun, but rarely seen at the top tables.

What the tiers don't tell you

A tier list ranks decks against the whole field. It doesn't say which deck is best against yours. For that, see our Goat matchup chart: every top deck at a glance, how to beat it, and who is favored in each matchup.
