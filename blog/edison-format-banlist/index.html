Edison Format Banlist Explained: The March 2010 List

November 23, 2026 · Draft

Edison format uses the TCG banlist from March 1, 2010, the list that was in effect at the 75th Shonen Jump Championship in Edison, New Jersey. Like every retro format, it never changes. It has 43 Forbidden cards, 70 Limited and 20 Semi-Limited.

The Goat Control engine is gone

The biggest change from Goat format: the cards that made Goat Control are Forbidden or Limited.

  • Forbidden: Metamorphosis, Thousand-Eyes Restrict, Tsukuyomi, Magician of Faith, Black Luster Soldier - Envoy of the Beginning, Pot of Greed, Graceful Charity, Snatch Steal, Ring of Destruction, Premature Burial, Delinquent Duo.
  • Limited: Scapegoat, Chaos Sorcerer, Sangan, Mirror Force, Torrential Tribute, Heavy Storm, Call of the Haunted.

So the old way of grinding one card at a time with flip effects is mostly gone, and Synchro Monsters take its place.

Limited Synchros and their helpers

Some of the format's strongest cards are one per deck:

  • Black Rose Dragon: destroys every card on the field when it is Synchro Summoned.
  • Goyo Guardian: steals the monster it destroys in battle.
  • Brionac, Dragon of the Ice Barrier: discards cards from your hand to return the same number of cards on the field to the hand.
  • Dark Armed Dragon: destroys a card each time you banish a DARK from your Graveyard.
  • Blackwing - Gale the Whirlwind and Plaguespreader Zombie: the Tuners that make Synchros easy.

Semi-Limited: the deck engines

Two copies each. These are the cards that hold the top decks together:

  • Black Whirlwind: searches a Blackwing every time one is Normal Summoned.
  • Treeborn Frog: comes back from the Graveyard every turn, the heart of the Frog decks.
  • Judgment Dragon: Lightsworn's board wipe.
  • Dandylion and Lonefire Blossom: the Plant engine.
  • Royal Oppression, Royal Decree and Skill Drain: the lock Traps.
  • Also: Bottomless Trap Hole, Cyber Dragon, Honest, Gold Sarcophagus, Magical Stone Excavation, Destiny HERO - Malicious, Goblin Zombie, Chain Strike, Demise, King of Armageddon, Ultimate Offering and United We Stand.

Still Forbidden from the old days

The cards that were too strong in 2005 mostly stayed that way: Chaos Emperor Dragon - Envoy of the End, Raigeki, Dark Hole, Monster Reborn, Change of Heart, Harpie's Feather Duster, Yata-Garasu and more. Newer additions include Crush Card Virus, Destiny HERO - Disk Commander, Dark Strike Fighter, Cyber-Stein, Victory Dragon and Dimension Fusion.
