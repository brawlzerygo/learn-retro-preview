**DuelingBook** is a browser site for playing Yu-Gi-Oh! online against real people. It took over from Dueling Network, which closed in 2016, and today it is the main place Goat format players duel. If you are learning a retro format, it is the easiest way to start playing.

## Manual play: what that means

DuelingBook doesn't play the game for you. You move every card yourself: draw, summon, activate, send to the Graveyard. Nothing stops an illegal play automatically, so you and your opponent keep each other honest. That sounds like a lot, but it is exactly like playing in person, and it means old rulings work the way they did back then.

Typing shortcuts like `/draw` and `/mill` speed up common actions.

## Step 1: Make an account

Go to duelingbook.com and register. You can also sign in with a Google account; if no DuelingBook account uses that email, it asks you to register one and links the two.

## Step 2: Build or import a deck

Open the **Deck Constructor**. You can build a deck card by card, or import a list you already have: DuelingBook reads YDK, YDKe and XML deck files. Every deck on our [Goat deck page](/goat/decks/) and [Edison deck page](/edison/decks/) has a Download .ydk button, so you can import it here in seconds.

DuelingBook's Goat card filter shows only Goat-legal cards, but it doesn't apply the April 2005 banlist for you. Check your deck against the [Goat banlist](/goat/banlist/) before you play.

## Step 3: Find a Goat game

DuelingBook has a **Goat Format** room in its Duel Room, which only allows cards released before September 2005. Host a duel there or join one.

- **Watch first:** in the Goat Format room, switch from duel to watch to see other people's games. It is one of the fastest ways to learn.
- **Find opponents:** the Goat community's Discord has a channel for finding games, and tells new players to say they are new and ask questions there.
- **Review your games:** DuelingBook saves replays of your duels, so you can go back and see where a game turned.

## Rated and unrated games

In rated games you can call a judge to settle a ruling, and appeal it to a senior administrator, whose call is final. Cheating or leaving a game can get your account frozen or banned. Unrated games skip all that, which makes them the easy place to start.

## What about Edison?

DuelingBook's own pages don't list an Edison room. Edison players have other options:

- **Dueling Nexus**, another free browser site, has an Edison ranked mode.
- **EDOPro**, a free automated simulator for Windows, Mac, Linux and Android, plays the rules for you and has a GOAT banlist built in.
- Edison events and opponents are posted by the Edison community at edisonformat.com.
