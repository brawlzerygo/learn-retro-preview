**DuelingBook** is a website for playing Yu-Gi-Oh! online against other people. It describes itself as the replacement for Dueling Network, and goatformat.com calls it the only viable and popular simulator for Goat format.

## Manual play: what that means

DuelingBook doesn't play the game for you. You and your opponent do every action yourselves: draw, summon, activate, send to the Graveyard, just like playing with real cards. Typed commands like `/draw` and `/mill` are shortcuts for common actions.

## Step 1: Make an account

Go to duelingbook.com and register. You can also sign in with a Google account; if no DuelingBook account uses that email, it asks you to register one and links the two.

## Step 2: Build or import a deck

Open the **Deck Constructor**. You can build a deck card by card, or import a list you already have: DuelingBook reads YDK, YDKe and XML deck files. Every deck on our [Goat deck page](/goat/decks/) and [Edison deck page](/edison/decks/) has a Download .ydk button, and you can import that file here.

Check your deck against the [Goat banlist](/goat/banlist/) or the [Edison banlist](/edison/banlist/) before you play.

## Step 3: Find a Goat game

DuelingBook has a **Goat Format** room in its Duel Room, which only allows cards released before September 2005. Host a duel there or join one.

- **Watch first:** in the Goat Format room, switch from duel to watch to see other people's games. goatformat.com recommends watching games as a way to learn.
- **Find opponents:** the Goat community's Discord has a channel for finding games, and tells new players to say they are new and ask questions there.
- **Review your games:** DuelingBook saves replays of your duels, so you can go back and see where a game turned.

## Rated and unrated games

In rated games you can call a judge to settle a ruling, and appeal it to a senior administrator, whose call is final. Cheating or going AFK in a game can be penalized, and breaking the site's rules can get an account frozen or banned. Unrated games are there too.

## What about Edison?

DuelingBook's own pages don't list an Edison room. Edison players have other options:

- **Dueling Nexus**, another site for online duels, has an Edison ranked mode.
- **EDOPro**, a free, open-source simulator for Windows, Mac, Linux and Android, applies the rules automatically. Its built-in banlists include Goat's, but not Edison's.
- edisonformat.com posts news about Edison events.
