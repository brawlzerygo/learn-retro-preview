If you played Yu-Gi-Oh! in the 2000s and are thinking about coming back, a lot has changed. But the version you remember can still be played, and there are several ways back in.

## What changed since you played

The biggest rule changes, in order:

- **Synchro Monsters:** a Tuner and other monsters whose Levels add up summon a Synchro Monster from the Extra Deck. Edison format is from this era.
- **Xyz Monsters:** monsters with the same Level summon an Xyz Monster from the Extra Deck.
- **Pendulum Monsters (TCG, July 2014):** monsters that can also be placed as Spells in their own zones. The same rule change ended the first-turn draw: the player going first no longer draws.
- **Link Monsters (TCG, July 2017):** a new kind of Extra Deck monster, plus Extra Monster Zones that limited where Extra Deck monsters could go.
- **Since then:** that limit was loosened, so Fusion, Synchro and Xyz Monsters can go to any Main Monster Zone again.

## Are your old cards still legal?

Yes. Konami's rule is that a card is legal once it is released in a TCG product, and a reprint doesn't change that. Your old English cards still count, along with French, Italian, German, Spanish and Portuguese ones. Japanese, Korean and other OCG cards aren't legal at TCG events.

## Other ways back in

- **Yu-Gi-Oh! Master Duel:** Konami's free-to-play digital game, released in January 2022.
- **DuelingBook:** a website where you play the card game against other people, with a room for Goat format. See [How to Play on DuelingBook](/blog/how-to-play-yugioh-on-duelingbook/).

## Or play the game you remember

**Retro formats** bring back the game as it was at one moment, with the cards, banlist and rules of that time. Nothing new is added, so you never need to keep up with new sets.

- **Goat format** is the game in summer 2005: slow and strategic, with long games. If you played around 2005, start here. Start with [What Is Goat Format?](/blog/what-is-goat-format/)
- **Edison format** is the game in spring 2010, with Synchro Monsters. If you played during 5D's, start with [What Is Edison Format?](/blog/what-is-edison-format/)

Konami supports this too: at **Time Wizard** events, an Official Tournament Store picks a date, and the cards, banlist and rules match that date. And Konami's own newer format, **Genesys** (since September 2025), uses a point system to build decks and leaves out Link and Pendulum Monsters.

## Your first steps

1. Pick a format: [Goat vs Edison](/blog/goat-vs-edison/) helps you choose.
2. Learn it from the basics with a free course: [Goat](/goat/course/) or [Edison](/edison/course/). Both start at the very beginning, for players who have been away for years.
3. Build a first deck: [Best Beginner Decks for Goat and Edison](/blog/best-beginner-decks-goat-edison/).
