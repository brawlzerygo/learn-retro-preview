Your first retro deck should do two things: win some games, and teach you how the format works. The decks below come up again and again when experienced players are asked what a new player should build. Each one links to a full 40-card list on this site.

## Goat format

### Goat Control

YGOPRODeck's introduction to Goat says it plainly: if you are new to the format, play Goat Control. It is the deck the format is named after, and it is also one of the strongest.

- **What it does:** it trades cards one for one and slowly pulls ahead. **Scapegoat** gives you four tokens, and **Metamorphosis** turns one of them into a big Fusion monster from the Fusion Deck. **Magician of Faith** gets back your best Spell, and **Tsukuyomi** and **Book of Moon** turn it face-down so you can flip it and use it again.
- **Why it's a good first deck:** almost every Goat game is about card advantage, and this deck teaches that better than any other. The same guide warns that it is hard to play perfectly. Take that as a good sign: you can keep getting better with it for a long time.
- **Full list:** [Goat Control](/goat/decks/goat-control/), a top-8 list from the 2020 Goat Format World Championship.

### A word on "fun" decks

Burn, Empty Jar and Exodia-style decks win in unusual ways, and new players often find them tempting. YGOPRODeck's advice is to try them for fun but not to make them your main deck, because they teach you less about how the format really works. If you want to see one anyway, start with [Burn](/goat/decks/burn/).

## Edison format

### Machina Gadget

Ultimate Guard's Edison guide names Machina Gadget as one of two decks that are ideal for beginners, and one of the cheapest to build.

- **What it does:** the three Gadgets (Red, Green and Yellow) each add the next Gadget to your hand when they are summoned, so the deck keeps feeding itself monsters. **Machina Fortress** can be Special Summoned by discarding Machines, and **Cyber Dragon** comes out for free when your opponent has a monster and you don't.
- **Why it's a good first deck:** the plan is simple and the deck rarely runs out of monsters, so you can focus on learning when to attack, when to hold back and when to use your Traps.
- **Full list:** [Machina Gadget](/edison/decks/machina-gadget/).

### Dragons

The same guide names Dragons as the other beginner pick. Our deck lists have two Dragon builds:

- [Dragon Aggro](/edison/decks/dragon-aggro/): **Red-Eyes Darkness Metal Dragon** and **Red-Eyes Wyvern** bring Dragons back from the Graveyard, and **Debris Dragon**, a Tuner, turns them into Dragon Synchro Summons. A fair, attacking deck with plenty of Traps.
- [Dragon Turbo](/edison/decks/dragon-turbo/): the same engine pushed harder, with more drawing and discarding to fill the Graveyard fast. Save it until you are comfortable with the format.
