Picking a first deck in a format you don't know yet is hard. The decks below are the ones published guides recommend for new players. Each one links to a full 40-card list on this site.

## Goat format

### Goat Control

YGOPRODeck's introduction to Goat says it plainly: if you are new to the format, play Goat Control. It is the deck the format is named after, and goatformat.com's 2020 tier list puts it in tier A.

- **What it does:** it plays a long game and wins on card advantage. **Scapegoat** summons four Sheep Tokens, and **Metamorphosis** Tributes one of them to summon **Thousand-Eyes Restrict** from the Fusion Deck. **Magician of Faith** adds a Spell from your Graveyard to your hand when flipped, and **Tsukuyomi** and **Book of Moon** turn it face-down so you can flip it again.
- **Why it's recommended:** YGOPRODeck calls it the best deck in the format, with the highest skill ceiling. The same guide warns that it is hard to play correctly.
- **Full list:** [Goat Control](/goat/decks/goat-control/), the 7th-place list from the 2020 Goat Format World Championship.

### A word on "fun" decks

Exodia, Empty Jar, Last Turn and Burn are alternate-win decks: they win without beating you in battle. YGOPRODeck says they appeal to newer players, but advises against playing them regularly. If you want to see one anyway, start with [Burn](/goat/decks/burn/).

## Edison format

### Machina Gadget

Ultimate Guard's Edison guide names Machina Gadget as one of two decks it calls ideal for beginners, both cheap to build.

- **What it does:** the three Gadgets (Red, Green and Yellow) each add the next Gadget to your hand when they are summoned, so the deck keeps feeding itself monsters. **Machina Fortress** Special Summons itself when you discard Machines whose Levels add up to 8 or more, and **Cyber Dragon** Special Summons itself when only your opponent controls a monster. The list backs them up with 11 Traps.
- **Full list:** [Machina Gadget](/edison/decks/machina-gadget/).

### Dragons

The same guide names Dragons as the other beginner pick, without a specific list. Our deck lists have two Dragon builds:

- [Dragon Aggro](/edison/decks/dragon-aggro/): **Red-Eyes Darkness Metal Dragon** Special Summons a Dragon from your hand or Graveyard once per turn, **Red-Eyes Wyvern** brings a Red-Eyes monster back from the Graveyard, and **Debris Dragon** is a Tuner for Dragon Synchros. The list runs 11 Traps.
- [Dragon Turbo](/edison/decks/dragon-turbo/): the same Red-Eyes Darkness Metal Dragon and Debris Dragon, with draw Spells like Trade-In and Cards of Consonance and no Traps at all.
