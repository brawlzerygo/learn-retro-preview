Goat format uses the TCG banlist from **April 2005**, exactly as it was. Unlike the modern list, it never changes: a deck that is legal today stays legal forever. Here is the whole list, and what the most important cards on it do.

## Forbidden (17 cards)

You can't play any copies of these:

- **Monsters:** Chaos Emperor Dragon - Envoy of the End, Fiber Jar, Magical Scientist, Makyura the Destructor, Witch of the Black Forest, Yata-Garasu.
- **Spells:** Butterfly Dagger - Elma, Change of Heart, Confiscation, Dark Hole, Harpie's Feather Duster, Mirage of Nightmare, Monster Reborn, Painful Choice, Raigeki, The Forceful Sentry.
- **Traps:** Imperial Order.

### Why these cards

- **Raigeki, Dark Hole and Harpie's Feather Duster** each wipe a whole side of the field for one card. In a format built on trading one card for one, a single card that takes out three or four is too much.
- **Chaos Emperor Dragon** clears every card in both hands and on the field, then burns for each one. Its partner, Black Luster Soldier - Envoy of the Beginning, is Limited, not Forbidden.
- **Yata-Garasu** stops your opponent from drawing after it deals damage. Once they are behind, it can lock them out of the game.
- **Monster Reborn and Change of Heart** bring back or take your opponent's best monster for one card, with no conditions.

## Limited (41 cards)

One copy each. Some of the most important:

- **The power monsters:** [Black Luster Soldier - Envoy of the Beginning](/goat/cards/black-luster-soldier-envoy-of-the-beginning/), Breaker the Magical Warrior, Jinzo, Sangan, Tribe-Infecting Virus, Exiled Force, D.D. Warrior Lady, Injection Fairy Lily.
- **The draw cards:** Pot of Greed and Graceful Charity.
- **Removal and theft:** Snatch Steal, Heavy Storm, Mystical Space Typhoon, Premature Burial, Delinquent Duo, Lightning Vortex.
- **The big Traps:** Mirror Force, Ring of Destruction, Torrential Tribute, Call of the Haunted, Magic Cylinder, Ceasefire.
- **Exodia:** Exodia the Forbidden One and its four pieces are one copy each.

The full Limited list: Black Luster Soldier - Envoy of the Beginning, Breaker the Magical Warrior, Cyber Jar, Dark Magician of Chaos, D.D. Warrior Lady, Exodia the Forbidden One, Left Arm, Right Arm, Left Leg and Right Leg of the Forbidden One, Exiled Force, Injection Fairy Lily, Jinzo, Morphing Jar, Protector of the Sanctuary, Reflect Bounder, Sacred Phoenix of Nephthys, Sangan, Sinister Serpent, Tribe-Infecting Virus, Twin-Headed Behemoth, Card Destruction, Delinquent Duo, Graceful Charity, Heavy Storm, Lightning Vortex, Mage Power, Mystical Space Typhoon, Pot of Greed, Premature Burial, Snatch Steal, Swords of Revealing Light, United We Stand, Call of the Haunted, Ceasefire, Deck Devastation Virus, Magic Cylinder, Mirror Force, Reckless Greed, Ring of Destruction, Torrential Tribute.

## Semi-Limited (15 cards)

Two copies each: Abyss Soldier, Dark Scorpion - Chick the Yellow, Manticore of Darkness, Marauding Captain, Night Assailant, Vampire Lord, Creature Swap, Emergency Provisions, Level Limit - Area B, Nobleman of Crossout, Reinforcement of the Army, Upstart Goblin, Good Goblin Housekeeping, Gravity Bind, Last Turn.

## What isn't on the list

The card the format is named after, **Scapegoat**, is unlimited in Goat: you can play three. So are Metamorphosis, Thousand-Eyes Restrict, Book of Moon and Tsukuyomi, the core of Goat Control. Five years later, Edison's March 2010 list had Forbidden Metamorphosis, Thousand-Eyes Restrict and Tsukuyomi, and Limited Scapegoat. See [Edison Format Banlist Explained](/blog/edison-format-banlist/).
