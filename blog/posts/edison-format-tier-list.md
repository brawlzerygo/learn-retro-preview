Edison format has a deep pool of real decks. Edison players sort them into five tiers: **Best**, **Competitive**, **Rogue**, **Contender** and **Casual**. Every deck below has a full list on our [Edison deck page](/edison/decks/), with its Main, Extra and Side Deck.

A tier tells you how a deck does against the whole field, not how fun it is or how hard it is to play. A good player with a Rogue deck beats an average player with a Best deck all the time.

## Best

The decks to expect at any Edison event.

- [Blackwing](/edison/decks/blackwing/): DARK birds that swarm the field with [Black Whirlwind](/edison/cards/black-whirlwind/) and turn spare birds into Synchros.
- [Vayu Turbo](/edison/decks/vayu-turbo/): Blackwings that start in the Graveyard. Blackwing - Vayu the Emblem of Honor makes Synchros straight from it.
- [Hybrid](/edison/decks/hybrid/): Blackwings with a heavier Trap line and lock cards like Royal Oppression.
- [Hero Frog](/edison/decks/hero-frog/) and [Junk Frog](/edison/decks/junk-frog/): Treeborn Frog comes back every turn as Tribute fodder for the Monarchs, with Elemental HEROes or Synchro tools on top.

## Competitive

Strong decks that win events when the field is right for them: [Diva Zombie](/edison/decks/diva-zombie/), [Diva Hero](/edison/decks/diva-hero/), [Diva Hero Beat](/edison/decks/diva-hero-beat/), [Dragon Turbo](/edison/decks/dragon-turbo/), [Lightsworn](/edison/decks/lightsworn/) and [Machina](/edison/decks/machina/).

Lightsworn is the one to watch: once four Lightsworn names are in the Graveyard, [Judgment Dragon](/edison/cards/judgment-dragon/) clears every other card on the field.

## Rogue

Decks that can beat the top tier on a good day: [Amaryllis](/edison/decks/amaryllis/), [Cat](/edison/decks/cat/), [Dragon Aggro](/edison/decks/dragon-aggro/), [Fairy](/edison/decks/fairy/), [Fish OTK](/edison/decks/fish-otk/), [Flamvell](/edison/decks/flamvell/), [Gladiator Beast](/edison/decks/gladiator-beast/), [Hero Beat](/edison/decks/hero-beat/), [Norleras](/edison/decks/norleras/) and [Quickdraw Plant](/edison/decks/quickdraw-plant/).

Quickdraw Plant has the biggest single turns in the format: Quickdraw Synchron, Dandylion and Debris Dragon can make two Synchros and a field of tokens from three cards.

## Contender

Solid decks a step behind: [Ancient Gear](/edison/decks/ancient-gear/), [Diva FLIP](/edison/decks/diva-flip/), [Gemini](/edison/decks/gemini/), [Gravekeeper](/edison/decks/gravekeeper/), [Hopeless Dragon](/edison/decks/hopeless-dragon/), [Kuraz](/edison/decks/kuraz/), [Machina Gadget](/edison/decks/machina-gadget/), [Machina Roid](/edison/decks/machina-roid/), [Quickdraw Volcanic](/edison/decks/quickdraw-volcanic/) and [TeleDAD](/edison/decks/teledad/).

Machina Gadget is one of the most recommended first decks: see [Best Beginner Decks for Goat and Edison](/blog/best-beginner-decks-goat-edison/).

Every one has a full list on the [deck page](/edison/decks/).
