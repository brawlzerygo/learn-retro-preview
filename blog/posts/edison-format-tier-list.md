edisonformat.net, the Edison community's site, sorts the format's decks into five tiers: **Best**, **Competitive**, **Rogue**, **Contender** and **Casual**. Every one of the 55 decks below has a full list on our [Edison deck page](/edison/decks/), with its Main, Extra and Side Deck.

## Best

- [Blackwing](/edison/decks/blackwing/): Blackwing monsters that search for more of themselves with [Black Whirlwind](/edison/cards/black-whirlwind/) and become Synchro material.
- [Vayu Turbo](/edison/decks/vayu-turbo/): Blackwings that start in the Graveyard. Blackwing - Vayu the Emblem of Honor banishes itself and another Blackwing from the Graveyard to Special Summon a Blackwing Synchro.
- [Hybrid](/edison/decks/hybrid/): Blackwings with Dark Grepher, Royal Oppression and Dark Bribe added.
- [Hero Frog](/edison/decks/hero-frog/) and [Junk Frog](/edison/decks/junk-frog/): [Treeborn Frog](/edison/cards/treeborn-frog/) Special Summons itself from the Graveyard during the Standby Phase as long as you control no Spells or Traps, so there is always a monster to Tribute for a Monarch like Caius the Shadow Monarch. Hero Frog adds Elemental HERO Absolute Zero; Junk Frog adds Junk Synchron for Synchros.

## Competitive

[Diva Zombie](/edison/decks/diva-zombie/), [Diva Hero](/edison/decks/diva-hero/), [Diva Hero Beat](/edison/decks/diva-hero-beat/), [Dragon Turbo](/edison/decks/dragon-turbo/), [Lightsworn](/edison/decks/lightsworn/) and [Machina](/edison/decks/machina/).

In Lightsworn, once four differently named Lightsworn monsters are in the Graveyard, [Judgment Dragon](/edison/cards/judgment-dragon/) can be Special Summoned, and for 1000 Life Points it destroys every other card on the field.

## Rogue

[Amaryllis](/edison/decks/amaryllis/), [Cat](/edison/decks/cat/), [Dragon Aggro](/edison/decks/dragon-aggro/), [Fairy](/edison/decks/fairy/), [Fish OTK](/edison/decks/fish-otk/), [Flamvell](/edison/decks/flamvell/), [Gladiator Beast](/edison/decks/gladiator-beast/), [Hero Beat](/edison/decks/hero-beat/), [Norleras](/edison/decks/norleras/) and [Quickdraw Plant](/edison/decks/quickdraw-plant/).

In Quickdraw Plant, Quickdraw Synchron Special Summons itself by sending a monster like Dandylion from your hand to the Graveyard, and Dandylion leaves two Fluff Tokens behind as Synchro material.

## Contender

[Ancient Gear](/edison/decks/ancient-gear/), [Diva FLIP](/edison/decks/diva-flip/), [Gemini](/edison/decks/gemini/), [Gravekeeper](/edison/decks/gravekeeper/), [Hopeless Dragon](/edison/decks/hopeless-dragon/), [Kuraz](/edison/decks/kuraz/), [Machina Gadget](/edison/decks/machina-gadget/), [Machina Roid](/edison/decks/machina-roid/), [Quickdraw Volcanic](/edison/decks/quickdraw-volcanic/) and [TeleDAD](/edison/decks/teledad/).

Ultimate Guard's Edison guide names Machina Gadget as a good first deck: see [Best Beginner Decks for Goat and Edison](/blog/best-beginner-decks-goat-edison/).
