If you are coming back to Yu-Gi-Oh!, or trying it for the first time without the speed of the modern game, two retro formats stand out: **Goat** and **Edison**. Format Library, a site that archives old formats and their decks, calls each of them one of the most popular retro formats. Both freeze the game at one moment, with a fixed card pool and a banlist that never changes. They froze it five years apart, and that gap makes them feel very different.

## The short version

- **Goat** is Yu-Gi-Oh! in the summer of 2005. Slow, control-heavy and long games. No Synchro Monsters.
- **Edison** is Yu-Gi-Oh! in the spring of 2010, with Synchro Monsters. Faster than Goat, but with few set opening combos.

## Side by side

| | Goat | Edison |
|---|---|---|
| Moment frozen | Summer 2005 | Spring 2010 |
| Newest core set | The Lost Millennium | Absolute Powerforce |
| Banlist | April 2005 | March 2010 |
| Extra monsters | Fusions only (the Fusion Deck) | Fusions and Synchros (the Extra Deck) |
| Pace | Slow, control-heavy | Faster, built around Synchro Summons |
| Named after | The card Scapegoat | The town of Edison, New Jersey, where a 2010 Shonen Jump Championship was played |

## How Goat feels

Goat is about card advantage. Getting more than one card ahead in a single turn is rare, and when it happens it can decide the game. The game is control-heavy, and a lot of the skill is reading what your opponent is holding. goatformat.com sums up the appeal: longer games that reward strategy more than fast opening combos.

## How Edison feels

Edison adds **Synchro Monsters**. Send a Tuner and other monsters whose Levels add up to the Graveyard, and a big monster like Black Rose Dragon or Stardust Dragon comes straight out of your Extra Deck. Powerful Synchros decide a lot of Edison games, and Ultimate Guard's guide calls the format fast-paced next to Goat. Still, YGOPRODeck notes that Edison decks have few set opening combos and that card advantage is hard to earn. Guides describe it as a middle ground between Goat and modern Yu-Gi-Oh!.

## Which one should you play?

- **Pick Goat** if you want a slow, strategic game, if you played around 2005, or if you like control decks and long games.
- **Pick Edison** if you want Synchro Monsters and a faster game, or if you played in the 5D's era around 2010.
- **Can't decide?** Both courses start from the very basics, so you can learn either one first and try the other later.
