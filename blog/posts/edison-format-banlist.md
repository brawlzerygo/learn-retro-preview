Edison format uses the TCG banlist from **March 1, 2010**, the list that was in effect at the 75th Shonen Jump Championship in Edison, New Jersey. Like every retro format, it never changes. It has 43 Forbidden cards, 70 Limited and 19 Semi-Limited.

## The Goat Control engine is gone

The biggest change from Goat format: the cards that made Goat Control are Forbidden or Limited.

- **Forbidden:** Metamorphosis, Thousand-Eyes Restrict, Tsukuyomi, Magician of Faith, Black Luster Soldier - Envoy of the Beginning, Pot of Greed, Graceful Charity, Snatch Steal, Ring of Destruction, Premature Burial, Delinquent Duo.
- **Limited:** Scapegoat, Chaos Sorcerer, Sangan, Mirror Force, Torrential Tribute, Heavy Storm, Call of the Haunted.

## Limited Synchros and their helpers

Several of the best-known Synchros and the cards that help make them are Limited:

- **[Black Rose Dragon](/edison/cards/black-rose-dragon/):** destroys every card on the field when it is Synchro Summoned.
- **[Goyo Guardian](/edison/cards/goyo-guardian/):** when it destroys a monster in battle, you can Special Summon that monster to your field.
- **[Brionac, Dragon of the Ice Barrier](/edison/cards/brionac-dragon-of-the-ice-barrier/):** discards cards from your hand to return the same number of cards on the field to the hand.
- **[Dark Armed Dragon](/edison/cards/dark-armed-dragon/):** banish a DARK from your Graveyard to destroy a card on the field.
- **[Blackwing - Gale the Whirlwind](/edison/cards/blackwing-gale-the-whirlwind/)** and **[Plaguespreader Zombie](/edison/cards/plaguespreader-zombie/):** Tuners that can Special Summon themselves.

## Semi-Limited: the deck engines

Two copies each. Several are the core of decks on our [Edison deck page](/edison/decks/):

- **[Black Whirlwind](/edison/cards/black-whirlwind/):** searches a Blackwing every time one is Normal Summoned.
- **[Treeborn Frog](/edison/cards/treeborn-frog/):** Special Summons itself from the Graveyard in your Standby Phase if you control no Spells or Traps. The Frog decks are built around it.
- **[Judgment Dragon](/edison/cards/judgment-dragon/):** with four differently named Lightsworn monsters in your Graveyard, it can be Special Summoned and, for 1000 Life Points, destroy every other card on the field.
- **[Dandylion](/edison/cards/dandylion/)** and **[Lonefire Blossom](/edison/cards/lonefire-blossom/):** Plants played in Quickdraw Plant and other Synchro decks.
- **[Royal Oppression](/edison/cards/royal-oppression/)**, **[Royal Decree](/edison/cards/royal-decree/)** and **[Skill Drain](/edison/cards/skill-drain/):** Traps that shut off Special Summons, Traps and monster effects.
- Also: Bottomless Trap Hole, Cyber Dragon, Honest, Gold Sarcophagus, Magical Stone Excavation, Destiny HERO - Malicious, Goblin Zombie, Chain Strike, Demise, King of Armageddon, Ultimate Offering and United We Stand.

## Still Forbidden from the old days

Many cards Forbidden in Goat format are still Forbidden here: Chaos Emperor Dragon - Envoy of the End, Raigeki, Dark Hole, Monster Reborn, Change of Heart, Harpie's Feather Duster, Yata-Garasu and more. Newer additions include Crush Card Virus, Destiny HERO - Disk Commander, Dark Strike Fighter, Cyber-Stein, Victory Dragon and Dimension Fusion.
