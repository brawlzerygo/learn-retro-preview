Goat Format Banlist Explained: Every Forbidden and Limited Card

November 23, 2026 · Draft

Goat format uses the TCG banlist from April 2005, exactly as it was. Unlike the modern list, it never changes: a deck that is legal today stays legal forever. Here is the whole list, and what some of the biggest cards on it do.

Forbidden (17 cards)

You can't play any copies of these:

  • Monsters: Chaos Emperor Dragon - Envoy of the End, Fiber Jar, Magical Scientist, Makyura the Destructor, Witch of the Black Forest, Yata-Garasu.
  • Spells: Butterfly Dagger - Elma, Change of Heart, Confiscation, Dark Hole, Harpie's Feather Duster, Mirage of Nightmare, Monster Reborn, Painful Choice, Raigeki, The Forceful Sentry.
  • Traps: Imperial Order.

What the biggest ones do

  • Raigeki destroys every monster your opponent controls, Dark Hole destroys every monster on the field, and Harpie's Feather Duster destroys every Spell and Trap your opponent controls. Each is one card.
  • Chaos Emperor Dragon - Envoy of the End pays 1000 Life Points to send every card in both hands and on the field to the Graveyard, then deals 300 damage for each card sent to your opponent's Graveyard.
  • Yata-Garasu makes your opponent skip their next Draw Phase each time it deals battle damage, and returns to your hand at the End Phase.
  • Monster Reborn Special Summons any monster from either Graveyard, and Change of Heart takes control of your opponent's monster until the End Phase.

Limited (41 cards)

One copy each. Of the 12 lists on our Goat deck page, all 12 play Pot of Greed and Graceful Charity, 10 play Heavy Storm, 9 play Snatch Steal, and 8 play Black Luster Soldier - Envoy of the Beginning. Exodia the Forbidden One and its four pieces are one copy each too.

The full Limited list: Black Luster Soldier - Envoy of the Beginning, Breaker the Magical Warrior, Cyber Jar, Dark Magician of Chaos, D.D. Warrior Lady, Exodia the Forbidden One, Left Arm, Right Arm, Left Leg and Right Leg of the Forbidden One, Exiled Force, Injection Fairy Lily, Jinzo, Morphing Jar, Protector of the Sanctuary, Reflect Bounder, Sacred Phoenix of Nephthys, Sangan, Sinister Serpent, Tribe-Infecting Virus, Twin-Headed Behemoth, Card Destruction, Delinquent Duo, Graceful Charity, Heavy Storm, Lightning Vortex, Mage Power, Mystical Space Typhoon, Pot of Greed, Premature Burial, Snatch Steal, Swords of Revealing Light, United We Stand, Call of the Haunted, Ceasefire, Deck Devastation Virus, Magic Cylinder, Mirror Force, Reckless Greed, Ring of Destruction, Torrential Tribute.

Semi-Limited (15 cards)

Two copies each: Abyss Soldier, Dark Scorpion - Chick the Yellow, Manticore of Darkness, Marauding Captain, Night Assailant, Vampire Lord, Creature Swap, Emergency Provisions, Level Limit - Area B, Nobleman of Crossout, Reinforcement of the Army, Upstart Goblin, Good Goblin Housekeeping, Gravity Bind, Last Turn.

What isn't on the list

The card the format is named after, Scapegoat, is unlimited in Goat: you can play three. So are Metamorphosis, Thousand-Eyes Restrict, Book of Moon and Tsukuyomi, the core of Goat Control. Five years later, Edison's March 2010 list had Forbidden Metamorphosis, Thousand-Eyes Restrict and Tsukuyomi, and Limited Scapegoat. See Edison Format Banlist Explained.
