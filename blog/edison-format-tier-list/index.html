Edison Format Tier List: Every Deck, Best to Casual

November 23, 2026 · Draft

edisonformat.net, the Edison community's site, sorts the format's decks into five tiers: Best, Competitive, Rogue, Contender and Casual. Every one of the 55 decks below has a full list on our Edison deck page, with its Main, Extra and Side Deck.

Best

  • Blackwing: Blackwing monsters that search for more of themselves with Black Whirlwind and become Synchro material.
  • Vayu Turbo: Blackwings that start in the Graveyard. Blackwing - Vayu the Emblem of Honor banishes itself and another Blackwing from the Graveyard to Special Summon a Blackwing Synchro.
  • Hybrid: Blackwings with Dark Grepher, Royal Oppression and Dark Bribe added.
  • Hero Frog and Junk Frog: Treeborn Frog Special Summons itself from the Graveyard during the Standby Phase as long as you control no Spells or Traps, so there is always a monster to Tribute for a Monarch like Caius the Shadow Monarch. Hero Frog adds Elemental HERO Absolute Zero; Junk Frog adds Junk Synchron for Synchros.

Competitive

Diva Zombie, Diva Hero, Diva Hero Beat, Dragon Turbo, Lightsworn and Machina.

In Lightsworn, once four differently named Lightsworn monsters are in the Graveyard, Judgment Dragon can be Special Summoned, and for 1000 Life Points it destroys every other card on the field.

Rogue

Amaryllis, Cat, Dragon Aggro, Fairy, Fish OTK, Flamvell, Gladiator Beast, Hero Beat, Norleras and Quickdraw Plant.

In Quickdraw Plant, Quickdraw Synchron Special Summons itself by sending a monster like Dandylion from your hand to the Graveyard, and Dandylion leaves two Fluff Tokens behind as Synchro material.

Contender

Ancient Gear, Diva FLIP, Gemini, Gravekeeper, Hopeless Dragon, Kuraz, Machina Gadget, Machina Roid, Quickdraw Volcanic and TeleDAD.

Ultimate Guard's Edison guide names Machina Gadget as a good first deck: see Best Beginner Decks for Goat and Edison.

Casual

Alien, Anti-Meta, Assault Mode, Batteryman, Black Salvo, Bushi, Cloudian, Codarus, Crystal Beast, Dark Gaia, Earthbound Immortal, Fortune Lady, Insect, Koa'ki Meiru, Neo-Spacian, Monster Mash, Morphtronic, Ojama, Psychic, Relinquished, Six Samurai, Spaceship, Uria and X-Saber. Every one has a full list on the deck page.
