Rules

How a duel works under the 2005 rules, in plain words. Start with "Goat at a glance" if you learned the game recently.

1Goat at a glance

Goat is played the way big tournaments played it in 2005. Cards are ruled as a head judge would have ruled them then, even where today's rulings differ. Anything not listed here works as it does in the modern game.

RuleIn Goat
First turnThe player going first draws in their first Draw Phase, but has no Battle Phase that turn.
Fusion DeckFusion monsters only, no size limit in 2005. No Extra Monster Zone.
Field SpellsOne face-up Field Spell for the whole table. Playing another destroys the one already there.
PriorityAfter a summon or a Chain resolves, the turn player may act first.
Damage StepSix steps, one Chain each, and only monster effects that negate an activation can be used.
MatchesBest of three. No match draws.

Source: goatformat.com, Rules and Policies and Goat Format Rules; the first-turn attack rule from Zack (2026-11-23)

3Priority and triggers

Priority: once a summon or a Chain finishes, the turn player gets the first chance to act: they may use a fast effect or a monster's Ignition effect before the opponent can answer.

Triggers at the same time: when several effects trigger together, they go on the Chain in the order the events happened, earlier ones first.

Replays: if the number of monsters your opponent controls changes after you declare an attack, you may choose a new target or stop. A new target counts as a fresh attack declaration.

Source: goatformat.com, Goat Format Rules (rules 4, 9 and 12)

4The Damage Step

The Damage Step in 2005 has six steps, one more than today, and only one Chain can happen in each. During it, the only cards that can be used are monster effects that negate an activation. Quick-Play Spells, Normal Traps and Continuous Trap effects all have to wait.

Source: goatformat.com, Goat Format Rules (rule 10)

5The June 2005 position rule

Before a rule change in June 2005, a monster that a card turned face-down could not be flipped face-up again that turn, because the move counted as its one position change. After the change it no longer counts. That is what lets decks turn their own Flip monsters face-down with cards like Book of Moon and Tsukuyomi and use them again, a big part of how Goat Control works. Standard Goat uses the rules after this change.

Source: YGOPRODeck, An Introduction to Goat Format

6Other 2005 rules

  • Two monsters with 0 ATK that battle in Attack Position are both destroyed.
  • You can't pay a Life Point cost that would take you to 0 or below, except for Chain Energy.
  • Embodiment of Apophis takes up both a Monster Zone and a Spell & Trap Zone.

Source: goatformat.com, Goat Format Rules (rules 15, 16 and 20)
