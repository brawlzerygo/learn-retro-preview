Glossary

Every term used in the course, in plain words.

Aggro

A deck style that attacks early and often to end the game before the opponent sets up.

Backrow

The Spells and Traps you have Set face-down.

Card advantage

Having more useful cards than your opponent, between your hand and your field.

Chain

Cards and effects played in reply to one another. The last one added resolves first.

Control

A deck style that answers everything the opponent plays and wins slowly with more cards.

Cost

What you give up to use a card, like a discard or Life Points. You do not get it back if the card is stopped.

Damage Step

The moment an attack actually hits. Very few cards can be used during it.

Flip effect

A monster effect that happens when the card is turned face-up, by a Flip Summon, an attack or a card.

Flip Summon

Turning your face-down monster face-up into Attack Position on a later turn.

Floater

A monster that brings out another monster or card when it leaves the field.

Fusion Deck

The 2005 name for the Extra Deck. It holds Fusion monsters, which you summon instead of drawing.

Fusion Summon

Using a card like Polymerization to send the listed monsters to the Graveyard and bring out a Fusion monster.

Graveyard (GY)

Your face-up discard pile. Anyone can look through it, and many cards use what is in it.

Life Points (LP)

The score you defend. Both players begin at 8000, and the first to hit 0 loses.

Lockdown

A deck style that stops the opponent from attacking or playing, then wins another way.

Main Deck

The 40 or more cards you shuffle and draw from.

Mill

Sending cards from the top of a Deck to the Graveyard.

Negate

To cancel a card or effect so it does nothing.

Normal Summon

Playing one monster from your hand face-up in Attack Position. You get one Normal Summon or Set each turn.

One-for-one

A trade where each player loses one card.

Plus one (+1)

A play that leaves you one card ahead of where you were, like Sangan trading with an attacker and then adding a monster from your Deck to your hand.

Priority

In 2005 rules, the turn player gets the first chance to act after a summon or a chain finishes.

Quick-Play Spell

A Spell you can use on either turn once it is Set, or from your hand on your own turn. Book of Moon and Scapegoat are two.

Remove from play

The 2005 wording for banish: the card goes to a pile outside the Graveyard and rarely comes back.

Set

Putting a card face-down. A Set monster sits in Defense Position. A Set Trap has to wait a turn before you can use it.

Sheep Token

A 0 ATK, 0 DEF monster made by Scapegoat. Four come out at once.

Side Deck

Either 0 or exactly 15 spare cards you may swap with Main or Fusion Deck cards between the games of a match.

Special Summon

Any summon a card lets you make. You can do as many as your cards allow.

Spell Speed

How fast a card or effect is, from 1 to 3. You can only reply with Speed 2 or more, and never with a slower card.

Target

To point an effect at one card. Effects that hit a whole group do not target.

Token

A monster created by a card effect. It has no real card and disappears when it leaves the field.

Tribute

Sending your own monster to the Graveyard to pay for a bigger one: one for Level 5 or 6, two for Level 7 or more.

Turn player

The player whose turn it is.
