Reading a Card

Monsters, Spells and Traps, and every part of a monster card. 7 min · 6 flashcards

Played Yu-Gi-Oh! before? You can skip the basics.Go to Goat 101 →

Three kinds of cards

Every card is a Monster, a Spell or a Trap. The color of the frame around the picture tells you which.

Card typeFrame colorDescriptionExample
MonsterSeveral colorsGoes on the field to attack and to block attacks.
SpellGreenDoes something when you play it. You use most of them on your turn.
TrapPurple-pinkPlaced face-down first, then sprung later, usually during your opponent's turn.

Monster frames

Monsters come in a few frame colors. Each color tells you something about how the monster works.

FrameColorDescription
NormalYellowNo effect. The text box is just a bit of flavor. is one.
EffectOrangeHas an ability written in its text box. is one.
FusionVioletLives in the Fusion Deck and is made from other monsters. is one.
RitualBlueNeeds a matching Ritual Spell to come out. Rare in Goat decks.

The parts of a monster

Open to follow along.

  • Name, across the top.
  • Attribute, the round symbol at the top right. There are six: DARK, LIGHT, EARTH, WATER, FIRE and WIND. Some cards care about Attributes, so they matter more than they look.
  • Level, the row of stars. Summoned Skull has 6. Higher Levels are harder to bring out, as lesson 0.4 shows.
  • Type, the word in square brackets at the top of the text box, such as [Fiend].
  • Text box, with the effect or the flavor text.
  • ATK and DEF at the bottom: how hard it hits, and how well it holds when attacked.
Think of it like this

ATK is the sword and DEF is the shield. Which one counts depends on the position the monster sits in, which lesson 0.5 explains.

Spells and Traps have kinds too

Under the name, a Spell or Trap shows a small icon for its kind. A card with no icon is a plain Normal Spell or Normal Trap.

KindDescription
NormalUsed once, then sent to the Graveyard.
Quick-Play SpellA Spell fast enough to use on either turn once it is Set.
ContinuousRemains face-up and its effect lasts.
Equip SpellAttaches to a monster and changes it.
Field SpellChanges the whole table. Only one can be face-up at a time.
Counter TrapThe fastest card in the game, made to stop other cards.
Old printings

Cards from the early years sometimes say "Magic Card" instead of Spell Card. They are the same thing.

Recap

  • Monsters fight. Green Spells act when played. Purple-pink Traps get Set and sprung later.
  • Yellow monsters have no effect, orange ones do, violet ones are Fusions.
  • Every monster shows its name, Attribute, Level, Type, text box, ATK and DEF.
  • Spells and Traps come in kinds like Quick-Play, Continuous and Equip.
