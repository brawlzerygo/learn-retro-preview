Goat Control

The deck the format is named after: Scapegoat, Metamorphosis and Thousand-Eyes Restrict. 10 min · 5 flashcards

What it wants

A long game: trade one for one, steal your best monster, reuse and pull ahead a card at a time.

Strengths

  • More quick-play protection than any other deck: Scapegoat, Book of Moon and a full Trap line.
  • It adapts to any opponent from the side deck.
  • The highest skill ceiling in the format: a good Goat player rarely throws a game away.

Weaknesses

  • It reacts more than it acts, so fast decks can win before it stabilizes.

The plan

Goat Control wins slowly. It answers everything, gets a little ahead each turn, and finishes once the opponent runs out of cards.

Spirit monsters

Asura Priest and Tsukuyomi are Spirits. They can't be Special Summoned, and they return to your hand in the End Phase of the turn they were Normal Summoned or flipped. That means you can use their effect again next turn.

How to play it

  1. Early on, Set monsters and Traps and trade one for one. Don't rush.
  2. Use Scapegoat on your opponent's turn, ideally in their End Phase.
  3. Attack with what you have, then bring out Restrict in Main Phase 2.
  4. Use Tsukuyomi and Book of Moon on your own Flip monsters to keep drawing and recycling Spells.

Strengths and weaknesses

goatformat.com's guide describes the deck as flexible and rewarding for skilled players, and still strong when the decks around it are aggressive. It also says other decks have since passed it as the best, partly because it reacts more than it acts.

Beating it

  • Asura Priest, or any card that hits every monster, clears Sheep Tokens.
  • Book of Moon or Ring of Destruction answer Restrict without battle.
  • Pressure early, before Restrict lands. Its flip-and-recycle loops need time.

Recap

  • Goat Control wins slowly by gaining cards and locking the board with Restrict.
  • Scapegoat, Metamorphosis and Restrict are the heart of it.
  • Flip monsters plus Book of Moon and Tsukuyomi keep it ahead.
  • Beat it with pressure, token clearing and non-battle answers to Restrict.
