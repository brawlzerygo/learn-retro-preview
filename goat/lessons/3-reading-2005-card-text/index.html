Reading 2005 Card Text

Old words, missing limits, and how to tell what a card did in 2005. 7 min · 5 flashcards

Old words, same meaning

Cards printed before 2006 use wording that has since changed. The cards still work the same way. Here is a translation table.

2005 wordingTodayDescription
Remove from playBanishThe card goes face-up to its own pile outside the Graveyard.
Magic CardSpell CardEarly printings only.
Fusion DeckExtra DeckThe pile your Fusion monsters wait in.
SelectTargetOld cards often say "select" where newer ones say "target".

Modern text can add limits

Over the years Konami reworded many old cards and sometimes added limits they didn't have in 2005. Goat plays cards the way they worked back then, so the newest text is not always the right one.

Example: Sangan

Today's text for says you can use its effect only once per turn, and you can't use the card you fetch for the rest of that turn. The 2005 card carries neither restriction. Every time Sangan leaves the field for the Graveyard, you search your Deck for any monster whose ATK is 1500 or lower and put it in your hand. In Goat, that is how it works.

When a card seems weaker than people say, check whether its modern text added something. The card pages on this site show the current wording from the card database, so treat any "once per turn" on an old card with care.

The everyday words

WordDescription
DestroyThe card is sent to the Graveyard by an effect or a battle. Some cards protect against this.
Send to the GraveyardMoves a card there without destroying it, so "when destroyed" effects don't trigger.
Remove from playAway from the Graveyard, where most cards can't reach it. does this.
Target or selectThe card points at one specific card. picks one Spell or Trap.
CostThe price of using a card, such as Life Points, a discard or a Tribute. It stays spent even if the card fails.
NegateCancels a card or effect so it does nothing.
CounterA marker placed on a card. gets a Spell Counter and can remove it to destroy a Spell or Trap.
Common mistakes
  • Reading "remove from play" as "destroy". The card skips the Graveyard entirely.
  • Applying a modern "once per turn" to an old card without checking.

Recap

  • Remove from play means banish. Magic Card means Spell Card. Fusion Deck means Extra Deck.
  • Goat uses how cards worked in 2005, so newer text can add limits that don't apply.
  • Destroy and send to the Graveyard are not the same thing.
  • Costs stay paid when a card is stopped.
