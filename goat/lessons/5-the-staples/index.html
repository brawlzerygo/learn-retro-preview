The Staples

The cards almost every Goat deck plays, and what each one does for you. 9 min · 6 flashcards

What makes a card a staple

A staple is a card that shows up in most decks no matter what those decks are trying to do. Goat has a lot of them because so many of the best cards are generic: they draw cards, remove anything, or steal monsters, so every deck wants them.

The counts below come from the 12 deck lists on this site, which are taken from goatformat.com's deck guides. A card counts once per deck that runs it.

All on the banlist

Pot of Greed, Graceful Charity, Heavy Storm, Snatch Steal, Delinquent Duo and Mystical Space Typhoon are Limited, so a deck runs one of each. Nobleman of Crossout is Semi-Limited. Book of Moon isn't on the list, so decks often play all three.

Ring of Destruction in 2005

Today's text only works on your opponent's turn, only on a monster whose ATK is no higher than their LP, and once a turn. The 2005 printing had none of those limits, and Goat plays the 2005 version. That makes it a finisher too: if both players are low, the player who survives the damage wins.

Sinister Serpent in 2005

Its 2005 text lets you return it from the Graveyard to your hand in each of your Standby Phases. That makes it free discard fodder for cards like Graceful Charity, and a Level 1 Tribute for Metamorphosis, again and again.

Why these cards and not others

Look at what they have in common. Pot of Greed and Graceful Charity make your hand better. Heavy Storm, Mystical Space Typhoon and Breaker clear Traps. Snatch Steal and Ring of Destruction handle the biggest monster on the field. Sangan and Sinister Serpent replace themselves. None of them care what deck you play, so every deck starts with them and builds around them.

Common mistakes
  • Using Heavy Storm while your own Set cards could be used first. Chain them before it resolves, or wait.
  • Playing Delinquent Duo into a hand of one card. It takes two only when they hold two or more.

Recap

  • A staple is a generic card that most decks play.
  • Pot of Greed and Graceful Charity are in all 12 site decks.
  • Most staples are Limited, so each deck plays one copy.
  • Ring of Destruction and Sinister Serpent work the 2005 way: no once-a-turn limits.
