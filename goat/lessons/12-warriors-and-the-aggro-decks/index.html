Warriors and the Aggro Decks

Warrior, Chaos Recruiter and Zombie: hit hard before the opponent sets up. 9 min · 5 flashcards

Aggro decks attack early and often. They want to end the game, or leave you too far behind, before a control deck can settle in.

Beating aggro

  • Traps that stop attacks and wipe the board, like Mirror Force and Torrential Tribute, hit aggro hardest.
  • Scapegoat buys turns.
  • Thousand-Eyes Restrict stops every attack while it stands.
  • Against Mystic Swordsman LV2, don't rely only on Set Flip monsters.

Recap

  • Aggro decks win by attacking before control decks settle.
  • Warrior uses Reinforcement of the Army to find the right attacker, backed by many Traps.
  • Chaos Recruiter and Zombie use monsters that summon more monsters when destroyed.
  • Board wipes, Scapegoat and Restrict slow aggro down.
