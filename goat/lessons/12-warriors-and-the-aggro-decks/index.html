Warriors and the Aggro Decks

Warrior, Chaos Recruiter and Zombie: hit hard before the opponent sets up. 9 min · 5 flashcards

Aggro decks attack early and often. They want to end the game, or leave you too far behind, before a control deck can settle in.

Warrior

See the lists: Warrior (Sean Montague, 4th place, Clash of Champions) and Chaos Warrior (Lukaz, winner of the first Patreon World Championship Qualifier, March 2021).

  • searches the right Warrior for the job.
  • destroys a face-down monster it attacks before damage, so Set Flip monsters never get to flip.
  • removes both itself and the monster it battled from play.
  • Tributes itself to destroy any monster.
  • gains 400 ATK when its owner holds one card or fewer, and when it is your only monster it stops the effects of Flip monsters it destroys in battle.
  • makes your opponent discard at random, or mills two cards, when it hits.
  • A heavy Trap line: Solemn Judgment, Trap Dustshoot and Sakuretsu Armor.

goatformat.com's guide notes the weak spot: Warriors gain few cards on their own, so they rely on winning battles.

Chaos Recruiter

See the list: Chaos Recruiter. and Mystic Tomato summon a new monster from the Deck when destroyed in battle, so every trade fills the Graveyard with LIGHT and DARK monsters for three Chaos Sorcerers and Black Luster Soldier. Asura Priest clears tokens, and because it goes back to the hand each turn, it dodges Mirror Force.

Zombie

See the list: Zombie, Delinkquent's winning list from Format Library Championship 17. and Giant Rat summon a new monster from the Deck when destroyed in battle, Pyramid Turtle reaching big Zombies like , Book of Life revives them, and three turn off the Traps that would stop the attacks.

Beating aggro

  • Traps that stop attacks and wipe the board, like Mirror Force and Torrential Tribute, hit aggro hardest.
  • Scapegoat buys turns.
  • Thousand-Eyes Restrict stops every attack while it stands.
  • Against Mystic Swordsman LV2, don't rely only on Set Flip monsters.

Recap

  • Aggro decks win by attacking before control decks settle.
  • Warrior uses Reinforcement of the Army to find the right attacker, backed by many Traps.
  • Chaos Recruiter and Zombie use monsters that summon more monsters when destroyed.
  • Board wipes, Scapegoat and Restrict slow aggro down.
