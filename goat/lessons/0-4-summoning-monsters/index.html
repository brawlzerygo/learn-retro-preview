Summoning Monsters

The Normal Summon, Tributes, Setting, flipping, Special Summons and Fusions. 9 min · 6 flashcards

Played Yu-Gi-Oh! before? You can skip the basics.Go to Goat 101 →

One Normal Summon a turn

Once per turn, in either Main Phase, you can put a monster from your hand onto the field, face-up and upright. This is called a Normal Summon. It is the main way monsters reach the field, and you only get one each turn, so pick it with care.

Bigger monsters need Tributes

Monsters of Level 4 or lower come out for free. Higher Levels ask you to send monsters you already control to the Graveyard first. That payment is called a Tribute.

LevelTributesExample
1 to 4NoneMost of your deck.
5 or 61 monster
7 or more2 monsters

A Tribute Summon still uses up your one Normal Summon for the turn.

Setting a monster

Instead of a Normal Summon, you can Set the monster: place it face-down, turned sideways, in Defense Position. Doing this spends your Normal Summon for the turn. Your opponent can see that something is there, but not what it is.

Flipping it up

On a later turn, you may turn your hidden monster face-up and upright, which is called a Flip Summon. It doesn't use your Normal Summon, but you can't do it on the same turn you Set the monster.

Some monsters have a Flip effect, marked "FLIP:" in the text. Any way the card ends up face-up sets it off: flipping it yourself, an attack on it, or another card's effect. destroys a monster when it flips.

Think of it like this

A Set Flip monster is a landmine. Attacking it is how your opponent steps on it.

Special Summons

When a card's text puts a monster onto the field, that is a Special Summon. There is no limit on these each turn: if your cards allow five of them, you can make five. Some strong monsters can only be Special Summoned, and their text tells you how.

Fusion Summons

Fusion monsters wait in your Fusion Deck. To bring one out you usually play , then send the exact monsters the Fusion lists from your hand or field to the Graveyard. The Fusion monster then comes out, and this counts as a Special Summon.

Common mistakes
  • Normal Summoning in face-up Defense Position. A Normal Summon is always face-up Attack. Defense means Setting it face-down.
  • Flip Summoning a monster the same turn you Set it.
  • Thinking a Tribute Summon is free of the once-a-turn limit. It is not.

Recap

  • Each turn: a single Normal Summon or Set.
  • Level 5 or 6 needs 1 Tribute, Level 7 or more needs 2.
  • A Flip Summon waits a turn. Flip effects trigger whenever the card turns face-up.
  • Special Summons have no limit. Fusions come from the Fusion Deck with cards like Polymerization.
