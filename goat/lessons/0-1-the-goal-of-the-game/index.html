The Goal of the Game

What Yu-Gi-Oh! is, how a game is won, and what each player brings. 6 min · 6 flashcards

Played Yu-Gi-Oh! before? You can skip the basics.Go to Goat 101 →

Two players, two decks

Yu-Gi-Oh! is a card game for two people. Each of you brings your own deck, built ahead of time from cards you chose. A single game is called a Duel.

You and your opponent take turns. On your turn you put cards on the table, attack with your monsters and try to break through whatever your opponent set up. On their turn, they do the same to you.

Life Points

Each player starts the Duel with 8000 Life Points, or LP for short. Think of LP as your health bar. Attacks and some card effects take LP away. Some cards give it back.

Think of it like this

A Duel is a race. You are trying to push your opponent's number down to zero before they do it to you, while also protecting your own.

How you win

There are three ways a Duel ends.

Way to winDescription
LP reach 0Your opponent's Life Points drop to zero. This is how nearly every Duel ends.
Empty DeckYour opponent needs to draw a card and has none left.
A card says soA few cards win on their own. wins if you hold it and its four limbs in your hand.

Your three decks

DeckSizeDescription
Main Deck40 or moreThe cards you shuffle and draw from. Most players use exactly 40.
Fusion DeckAny numberSpecial Fusion monsters that sit to the side. You never draw them. Lesson 0.4 shows how they come out.
Side Deck0 or 15Extra options kept aside, traded into your deck after a game ends.

Why 40 and not more? A smaller deck means you see your best cards more often. If you have one in 40 cards, you find it sooner than in 60.

Three is the most copies of one card a deck may hold. A list called the banlist lowers that for some cards. Goat 101 covers that list.

Starting the Duel

  1. Both players shuffle their Main Deck and set it face-down.
  2. Decide who goes first. A coin flip or a dice roll is the usual way.
  3. Each player draws 5 cards. That is your starting hand.
Newer than Goat?

Today's game calls the Fusion Deck the Extra Deck and puts more kinds of monsters in it. In Goat, it only ever holds Fusion monsters.

Recap

  • A Duel is two players, each with their own deck, taking turns.
  • Both start with 8000 LP and a hand of 5.
  • Win by taking your opponent to 0 LP, by them running out of cards to draw, or by a card like Exodia.
  • Main Deck of 40 or more, a Fusion Deck to the side, and a Side Deck of 0 or 15.
