Lockdown Burn

See the list: Lockdown Burn. It stops you attacking first: stops Level 4 and higher monsters, stops monsters with 1500 or more ATK, and Level Limit - Area B forces Level 4 and higher monsters into Defense Position. Then grows by 1000 damage for each of its owner's Standby Phases, and Nightmare Wheel, Lava Golem and Stealth Bird chip away.

Pressure helps too

None of these decks want a long fight against a fast opponent. Attacking early forces them to use their stall cards before they are ready.

Recap

  • Burn wins through effect damage. Lockdown Burn stops your attacks first.
  • Empty Jar makes you draw your whole Deck in one turn.
  • Reasoning Gate digs out Chaos monsters and wins in one turn, but breaks under disruption.
  • Trap and Spell stoppers, plus early pressure, beat them.
