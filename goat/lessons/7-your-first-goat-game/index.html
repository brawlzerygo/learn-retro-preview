Your First Goat Game

Seven turns of a real game, step by step, with the reasons behind each play. 12 min · 6 flashcards

Ana plays a Goat Control deck. Ben plays a Warrior deck. Both start at 8000 LP. Ana goes first. Every play below follows the 2005 rules from lessons 2 to 6, so try to call each move before you read it.

Turn 1: Ana

  1. Draw. Ana starts with , , , Sakuretsu Armor, and Breaker the Magical Warrior (6 cards).
  2. Main Phase 1. Pot of Greed draws Metamorphosis and Sangan (7 cards).
  3. She Sets Magician of Faith in Defense Position, then Sets Sakuretsu Armor and Scapegoat (4 cards left).
  4. No Battle Phase on the first turn. Ana ends with Book of Moon, Breaker, Metamorphosis and Sangan in hand.

Turn 2: Ben

  1. Ben draws (6 cards) and Normal Summons , 1600 ATK.
  2. He attacks the face-down monster. Ana flips Sakuretsu Armor and destroys Blade Knight before the attack lands.
  3. In Main Phase 2 Ben Sets two cards: Sakuretsu Armor and Call of the Haunted (3 cards left).

Turn 3: Ana

  1. Ana draws Nobleman of Crossout (5 cards).
  2. She Flip Summons Magician of Faith. Its Flip effect returns a Spell from her Graveyard to her hand. She picks Pot of Greed, plays it again and draws Graceful Charity and Mirror Force (7 cards).
  3. She Normal Summons Breaker (1900 ATK with its counter), then removes the counter to destroy one of Ben's Set cards: his Sakuretsu Armor. Breaker drops to 1600.
  4. Battle. Breaker attacks directly. Ben answers with Call of the Haunted, bringing Blade Knight back in Attack Position. The number of monsters changed, so Ana gets a replay: she can pick a new target or stop. Breaker and Blade Knight both have 1600 ATK, and she would rather keep Breaker, so she stops.
  5. Main Phase 2. Ana Sets Mirror Force, then plays Book of Moon on her own Magician of Faith. It was Flip Summoned this turn, so she couldn't change its position by hand, but a card effect can. Now it is face-down, ready to flip again (4 cards left).
The Goat Control loop

Magician of Faith brings back a Spell, and Book of Moon turns it face-down so it can do it again. Under the June 2005 rules, being turned face-down by a card doesn't use up the monster's position change. Goat Control is built on loops like this one.

Turn 4: Ben

  1. Ben draws (4 cards) and Normal Summons , then Tributes it to destroy Breaker.
  2. Blade Knight attacks the face-down Magician of Faith. Ana flips : Blade Knight is destroyed, and Call of the Haunted goes with it, since its monster left the field.
  3. Ben Sets one card and ends his turn (2 cards).
  4. In Ben's End Phase, Ana activates Scapegoat. Four Sheep Tokens arrive in Defense Position.
Why the End Phase?

Scapegoat stops you from summoning other monsters the turn you use it. Using it at the end of your opponent's turn costs you nothing, and the tokens are ready for your own turn.

Turn 5: Ana

  1. Ana draws Tsukuyomi (5 cards). She Flip Summons Magician of Faith again and gets Pot of Greed back once more. Pot of Greed draws Heavy Storm and Black Luster Soldier (7 cards).
  2. Heavy Storm destroys Ben's Set card, a second Sakuretsu Armor.
  3. Black Luster Soldier stays in her hand. Her Graveyard has a DARK monster (Breaker) but no LIGHT one yet.
  4. Battle. Ben has no monsters, so Magician of Faith attacks directly for 300. Ben is at 7700.
  5. Main Phase 2. Now : she Tributes one Sheep Token and brings out . She waited until after the Battle Phase, because once Restrict is out her other monsters can't attack (5 cards left).

Turn 6: Ben

  1. Ben draws (3 cards) and Normal Summons D.D. Warrior Lady. Restrict stops it from attacking.
  2. He Sets one card and ends his turn (1 card).

Turn 7: Ana

  1. Ana draws (6 cards). Restrict equips D.D. Warrior Lady and becomes 1500 ATK, 1600 DEF.
  2. Restrict attacks directly. Ben flips his Set card, Waboku, and takes no battle damage this turn.

Where the game stands

PlayerFieldHandLife Points
AnaRestrict (with D.D. Warrior Lady), Magician of Faith, 3 Sheep Tokens68000
BenNothing17700

After seven turns Ben has lost only 300 LP, yet he is far behind: one card in hand against six, and nothing on the field. That is a typical Goat game. The player with more cards nearly always wins in the end, and Ana's job now is to keep her lock and grind Ben down.

What Ben could have done better
  • Attacking into two Set cards on turn 4 walked into Mirror Force.
  • Playing his monsters one at a time into Ana's Traps gave her a one-for-one trade every turn while her Magician of Faith kept refilling her hand.

Recap

  • Magician of Faith and Book of Moon make a loop that reuses your best Spells.
  • Use Scapegoat at the end of your opponent's turn.
  • Attack first, then bring out Thousand-Eyes Restrict in Main Phase 2.
  • Count cards, not Life Points, to see who is winning.
