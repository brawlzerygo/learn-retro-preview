The Field and the Turn

Where every card goes, and the six phases each turn moves through. 7 min · 7 flashcards

Played Yu-Gi-Oh! before? You can skip the basics.Go to Goat 101 →

Your side of the table

The table is called the field. Each player has their own half with the same spaces, called zones.

ZoneNumberDescription
Monster Zones5Where your monsters go, face-up or face-down.
Spell & Trap Zones5Home for your Spells and Traps, shown or hidden.
Field Zone1For a Field Spell. Only one Field Spell can be face-up on the whole table.
Deck1 pileYour Main Deck, face-down. You draw off the top.
Graveyard1 pileFace-up pile for used and destroyed cards. Your opponent is free to check it too.
Fusion Deck1 pileFace-down, and only you may look at it.

Cards that are removed from play go face-up into a separate pile of their own, apart from the Graveyard. Lesson 3 of Goat 101 has more on that wording.

Think of it like this

The Graveyard is not the end of a card. Plenty of cards fish things back out of it. Removed from play is closer to gone for good.

A turn, phase by phase

Every turn walks through the same six phases. You can't go back to an earlier one.

  1. Draw Phase. Take the top card of your Deck.
  2. Standby Phase. A checkpoint. Any card that mentions the Standby Phase does its thing at this point. Most turns, nothing happens here.
  3. Main Phase 1. The busy part: summon monsters, play Spells, Set cards face-down.
  4. Battle Phase. Attack with your monsters if you want. You can skip it.
  5. Main Phase 2. A second chance to do Main Phase things, now that you have seen how the attacks went.
  6. End Phase. Effects that last "until the end of the turn" stop. A hand bigger than 6 cards gets trimmed back to 6.

Then the other player takes a full turn of their own, starting with their Draw Phase.

The very first turn

In Goat, the player who goes first does draw in their first Draw Phase, so they begin that turn with 6 cards. Many newer rule sets skip that draw. Goat keeps it. What they can't do is attack: there is no Battle Phase on the very first turn of the Duel.

What you can do in a Main Phase

  • Normal Summon or Set one monster. Just one per turn (lesson 0.4).
  • Special Summon, as many times as your cards allow.
  • Play Spells from your hand, and Set Spells and Traps face-down.
  • Change a monster's position, once per monster per turn.
  • Use monster effects that say you can.
Easy to forget
  • Main Phase 2 exists. Summoning after your attacks keeps your opponent guessing.
  • The hand limit is 6, checked in your End Phase.

Recap

  • Each side has 5 Monster Zones, 5 Spell & Trap Zones, a Field Zone, a Deck, a Graveyard and a Fusion Deck.
  • The phases run Draw, Standby, Main 1, Battle, Main 2 and End.
  • Going first still gets you a draw in Goat, but no attacks that turn.
  • End your turn with 6 cards or fewer in hand.
