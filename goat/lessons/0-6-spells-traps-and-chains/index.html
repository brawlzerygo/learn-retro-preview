Spells, Traps and Chains

Setting cards, springing Traps, and what happens when cards answer each other. 9 min · 6 flashcards

Played Yu-Gi-Oh! before? You can skip the basics.Go to Goat 101 →

Playing a Spell

In your Main Phase you can play a Spell straight from your hand: place it face-up in a Spell & Trap Zone and do what it says. A Normal Spell then goes to the Graveyard. Or place it face-down and save it.

Setting a Trap

A Trap can't be played from your hand. First you Set it face-down in your Main Phase. Starting with the next turn, you can flip it up whenever its condition happens, even in the middle of your opponent's turn.

Example

You Set . On your opponent's turn their monster attacks. You flip Sakuretsu Armor up, and it destroys the attacker before the attack lands.

Chains: cards answering cards

When a card is used, the other player gets a chance to answer it with a card of their own. Then the first player can answer that, and so on. This stack of answers is a Chain. Each card in it is a Chain Link.

Once neither player adds anything, the Chain resolves from the last link back to the first.

Example

Your opponent attacks and you flip (Chain Link 1). They answer with on your Mirror Force (Chain Link 2). The Chain resolves backwards: Mystical Space Typhoon destroys Mirror Force first. Mirror Force was already used, though, so it still resolves and destroys their Attack Position monsters. Destroying a card that is already in a Chain does not stop it. Only cards that say "negate" do that.

Spell Speed

Cards and effects are rated 1, 2 or 3 for speed. A reply has to be at least as fast as the card it answers.

SpeedCardsDescription
1Most Spells, most monster effectsCan start a Chain but never answer one.
2Quick-Play Spells, TrapsCan answer Speed 1 or 2.
3Counter TrapsCan answer anything. Nothing but another Counter Trap can reply to one.

So , a Quick-Play Spell, can answer your opponent's Trap Card, but a Normal Spell like can never be played in reply to anything.

Common mistakes
  • Springing a Trap the same turn you Set it. You must wait a turn.
  • Thinking destroying a used Trap stops it. Only negation stops it.
  • Answering a Trap with a Normal Spell. Too slow.

Recap

  • Spells can be played from the hand on your turn. Traps must be Set first and wait a turn.
  • Cards used in answer to each other form a Chain, which resolves last to first.
  • You can only answer with a card of equal or higher Spell Speed.
  • Destroying a card in a Chain does not stop it. Negating does.
