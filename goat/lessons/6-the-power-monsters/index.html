The Power Monsters

Black Luster Soldier, Chaos Sorcerer, Breaker and Thousand-Eyes Restrict. 9 min · 6 flashcards

Black Luster Soldier - Envoy of the Beginning

is a Level 8 with 3000 ATK. You can't Normal Summon it. Instead you Special Summon it from your hand by removing one LIGHT and one DARK monster in your Graveyard from play. No Tributes needed.

Once per turn it picks one of two things: remove a monster on the field from play (then it can't attack that turn), or, if it destroys a monster in battle, attack a second time in a row. It is Limited, and the YGOPRODeck introduction calls it the queen of the format.

Why LIGHT and DARK matter

Goat decks are built so their Graveyard fills with both: Sangan and Breaker are DARK, Magician of Faith and Shining Angel are LIGHT. That is why so many staples share those two Attributes.

Chaos Sorcerer

comes out the same way, for one LIGHT and one DARK from your Graveyard. It has 2300 ATK, and once per turn it removes a face-up monster from play, giving up its attack that turn. It isn't on the banlist, so decks built around it play two or three.

Breaker the Magical Warrior

gets a Spell Counter when it is Normal Summoned, which gives it 300 more ATK. Remove the counter to destroy one Spell or Trap. One card clears a Set card and still leaves a 1600 ATK monster on the field. It only gets the counter from a Normal Summon, so a Breaker that is Set and flipped later has no counter.

Thousand-Eyes Restrict

is a Level 1 Fusion with 0 ATK. Hardly anyone uses its Fusion materials. Instead they play : Tribute a Level 1 monster and Special Summon a Fusion of the same Level from the Fusion Deck. A Sheep Token from , Sinister Serpent or Magician of Faith all work.

Once it lands, no other monster on the field can attack or change its position, yours included. Once per turn it takes one of your opponent's monsters and equips it, copying that monster's ATK and DEF. If Restrict would be destroyed in battle, the equipped monster goes instead.

Think of it like this

Restrict is a wall that also steals. Your opponent loses a monster, can't attack with the rest, and has to find an answer that isn't battle.

Answering them

  • Answers outside of battle: Ring of Destruction, Snatch Steal and Book of Moon. Sakuretsu Armor only works when a monster declares an attack.
  • Book of Moon on Restrict turns it face-down, which ends the lock and drops the stolen monster to the Graveyard.
  • Black Luster Soldier and Chaos Sorcerer need a LIGHT and a DARK in the Graveyard. Cards that remove monsters from the Graveyard, like , slow them down.
Common mistakes
  • Attacking with your other monsters after summoning Thousand-Eyes Restrict. They can't. Attack first, then bring it out in Main Phase 2.
  • Setting Breaker and expecting a counter when it flips.

Recap

  • Black Luster Soldier and Chaos Sorcerer each need one LIGHT and one DARK removed from your Graveyard.
  • Black Luster Soldier removes a monster or attacks twice. Chaos Sorcerer removes a face-up monster.
  • Breaker destroys a Spell or Trap and stays as a 1600 ATK monster.
  • Thousand-Eyes Restrict comes from Metamorphosis, stops all other attacks and steals a monster.
