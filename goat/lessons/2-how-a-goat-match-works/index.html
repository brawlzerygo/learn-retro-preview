How a Goat Match Works

Best of three, siding, deck sizes and the 2005 rules that differ from today. 7 min · 6 flashcards

A match is best of three

At a tournament you don't play one Duel against an opponent, you play a match. Whoever takes two Duels first takes the match. Goat events don't use match draws: matches are played out to two wins.

Between games: the Side Deck

After the first Duel you may swap cards between your Side Deck and your Main or Fusion Deck, one for one, so your counts stay the same. This lets you bring in answers to what your opponent showed you. In 2005 rules the Side Deck is either empty or exactly 15 cards, though few events check that today.

Deck sizes in 2005

DeckSizeDescription
Main Deck40 or more2005 rules set no top limit, but DuelingBook stops at 60, and almost everyone plays 40.
Fusion DeckNo limitFusion monsters only. Online simulators may cap it.
Side Deck0 or 15Traded in after a Duel ends.

The 2005 rules that catch modern players

If you learned Yu-Gi-Oh! in recent years, a few things work differently in Goat. Here are the big ones.

RuleDescription
First turnThe player going first draws in their first Draw Phase, but can't attack that turn.
Fusion DeckThere is no Extra Monster Zone and no Synchro, Xyz, Pendulum or Link monsters. Fusions go to your regular Monster Zones.
One Field SpellOnly one Field Spell can be face-up for both players. Playing a second one wipes out the first.
PriorityAfter a summon or a Chain finishes, the turn player gets the first chance to act again.
The Damage StepVery strict. Almost nothing can be used while an attack is being worked out.
Old rulingsCards are ruled the way a head judge would have ruled them in 2005, even when today's rulings differ.
More in Goat 201

Priority, the order of triggers in a Chain and the Damage Step get their own lesson in Goat 201, lesson 8. For now it is enough to know they work a little differently.

Where to play

Most Goat games today happen online on DuelingBook, which has a Goat room and a deck builder limited to the Goat card pool. In person, local groups and the community's own events run on the same rules.

Common mistakes
  • Skipping your first draw out of habit when you go first.
  • Looking for an Extra Monster Zone. There isn't one.
  • Siding in a card without siding one out.

Recap

  • A match is won by the first player to win two Duels.
  • Side Deck cards come in after a Duel, each one replacing a card that goes out.
  • Main Deck 40 or more (60 online), Fusion Deck any size, Side Deck 0 or 15.
  • The first player draws, Fusions use regular zones, and 2005 rulings apply.
