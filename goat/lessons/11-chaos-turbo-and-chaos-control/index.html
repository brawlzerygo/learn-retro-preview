See the list: Chaos Turbo, Riccardo Lari's winning deck from the 2020 Goat Format World Championship. goatformat.com's news in 2026 calls Chaos Turbo the most popular deck in the format.

It plays no Scapegoat and no Metamorphosis. Instead it runs Flip monsters and cards that like being discarded, so its Graveyard fills with LIGHT and DARK monsters for and Black Luster Soldier.

See the lists: Chaos Control and Thunder Dragon Chaos Control, the second from Dale Bellido's 2nd place at the 2020 World Championship. Chaos Control is a blend: the Goat Control core of Scapegoat, Metamorphosis and Flip monsters, plus Chaos Sorcerer and Black Luster Soldier.

LIGHT and DARK monsters such as Sangan, Breaker, Tsukuyomi, Magician of Faith and do double duty. The game starts slow, then Chaos Sorcerer and Thousand-Eyes Restrict take over. The guide notes the deck has trouble with an opponent's Scapegoat, which is why a single Asura Priest is in the list.

Playing against Chaos decks

  • Watch their Graveyard. No LIGHT or no DARK means no Chaos monster this turn.
  • and other cards that remove Graveyard monsters cut off their fuel.
  • Trap Dustshoot can catch a Chaos monster in their hand before it lands.
  • Against Chaos Turbo, keep your hand small enough that its discard Traps hurt less, and remember Raigeki Break hits Set cards too.

Recap

  • Chaos Turbo fills its Graveyard with Thunder Dragon and Flip monsters, then lands Chaos monsters.
  • Raigeki Break turns spare cards into removal.
  • Chaos Control adds the Chaos monsters to the Goat Control core.
  • Starve both of LIGHT and DARK Graveyard monsters.
