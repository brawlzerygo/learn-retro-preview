The Card Pool and the Banlist

Which cards are legal, and the April 2005 Forbidden and Limited List. 7 min · 6 flashcards

The card pool

Goat follows a single cutoff: every card that was legal at big tournaments on August 17, 2005. That takes in every main set from Legend of Blue Eyes White Dragon through The Lost Millennium, plus the starter decks and promo cards of that time. Exarion Universe and Cybernetic Revolution came out around then but are not legal in the standard version of Goat.

Other versions exist

Some groups play a version with Exarion Universe added, and a few others. This site teaches the standard one, which goatformat.com and DuelingBook use.

Classics like are in. Nothing from 2006 or later is. The Card search on this site holds only Goat-legal cards, so anything you find there is in the pool.

Three limits

StatusCopiesDescription
Forbidden0Can't be played at all.
Limited1One copy in your Main, Fusion and Side Deck combined.
Semi-Limited2Up to two copies.
Unlimited3Every card not on the list.

The April 2005 list

Goat uses the official TCG list that took effect in April 2005, unchanged: 17 Forbidden cards, 41 Limited and 15 Semi-Limited. The full list is on the Banlist page.

A few Forbidden cards stand out because they are famous elsewhere: , and are all off the table. That shapes the whole format: without them, wiping the board or bringing a monster back takes much more work.

Limited cards show up in nearly every deck at one copy. draws 2 cards for nothing, and draws 3 and discards 2. , which removes a Set monster from play, is Semi-Limited.

Think of it like this

The Limited list is a shopping list. Most Goat decks pick up the same one-of power cards first, then fill the rest of the deck with their own plan.

Recap

  • Goat-legal cards are those legal on August 17, 2005: through The Lost Millennium, not Exarion Universe or Cybernetic Revolution.
  • Forbidden is 0 copies, Limited 1, Semi-Limited 2, everything else 3.
  • The April 2005 list has 17 Forbidden, 41 Limited and 15 Semi-Limited cards.
  • Monster Reborn, Dark Hole and Raigeki are Forbidden.
