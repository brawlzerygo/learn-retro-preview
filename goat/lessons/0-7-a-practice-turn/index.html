A Practice Turn

Two short turns played step by step, using everything from Step 0. 8 min · 5 flashcards

Played Yu-Gi-Oh! before? You can skip the basics.Go to Goat 101 →

Two players, Ana and Ben, start a Duel. Both have 8000 LP. Ana won the coin flip and goes first. Follow each step and check it against the earlier lessons.

Turn 1: Ana

  1. Draw Phase. Ana draws, so she has 6 cards: , , , , and a second Sakuretsu Armor.
  2. Main Phase 1. She plays Pot of Greed from her hand and draws 2 cards. Pot of Greed goes to her Graveyard. She now has 7 cards.
  3. She uses her Normal Summon to Set Man-Eater Bug face-down in Defense Position. If Ben attacks it, its Flip effect will destroy a monster.
  4. She Sets one Sakuretsu Armor face-down in her Spell & Trap Zone. From Ben's turn on, it can stop an attack.
  5. No Battle Phase. This is the first turn of the Duel, so Ana can't attack. Her monster is face-down in Defense Position anyway.
  6. End Phase. Ana counts her hand: 5 cards. That is under 6, so she keeps them all.

Turn 2: Ben

  1. Draw Phase. Ben draws his 6th card.
  2. Main Phase 1. Ben Normal Summons a Level 4 monster with 1800 ATK in Attack Position.
  3. Battle Phase. He sees one face-down monster and one face-down Spell or Trap. He decides to attack the Set monster.
  4. Ana flips Sakuretsu Armor in response to the attack. It destroys Ben's attacker. The attack never happens, and her Man-Eater Bug stays hidden for later.
  5. Main Phase 2. Ben has used his Normal Summon, so he Sets two cards in his Spell & Trap Zones and ends his turn.
What Ben could have done

Ben knew a face-down card could be a Trap. Some players hold back and Set a monster of their own instead of attacking into an unknown board. Goat is full of decisions like this one, and Goat 201 teaches how to weigh them.

Reading the board after turn 2

Nobody has lost LP yet, but Ana is ahead. She traded one Trap for Ben's monster, and she still has a hidden monster and more cards in hand. In Goat, being ahead on cards is often worth more than being ahead on Life Points.

Recap

  • Draw, then use your Main Phase to summon or Set and to place your Spells and Traps.
  • A Set monster and a Set Trap make your opponent guess.
  • A Trap can stop an attack before it hits.
  • Count cards, not just Life Points, to see who is ahead.
