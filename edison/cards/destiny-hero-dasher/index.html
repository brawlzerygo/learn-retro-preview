Destiny HERO - Dasher

Warrior / Effect · DARK · Level 6 · 2100 ATK / 1000 DEF

Banlist: Unlimited (3 copies)

Edison text

No Edison-Accurate PSCT available for this card.

Printed text (newer wording, may work differently)
Once per turn: You can Tribute 1 other monster; this card gains 1000 ATK until the End Phase. If this card attacks, change it to Defense Position at the end of the Battle Phase. One time only, when you draw a Monster Card during your Draw Phase while this card is in your GY: You can reveal it; Special Summon it. This card must be in the GY to activate and to resolve this effect.

Edison rulings

●The Ignition Effect Tributes 1 monster you control as cost. ●If Soul Exchange targeted your opponent's monster, you can Tribute it for this effect. ●You can't Tribute more than 1 monster by this effect. ●Dasher can't Tribute itself for this effect. ●The Continuous Effect does not activate, it applies at the end of the Battle Phase. ●The optional Trigger Effect follows a When... you can structure and can miss the timing. ●This effect starts a chain by revealing the drawn Monster Card. ●Dasher must be in the GY in order to activate this effect, but not to resolve it. ●If this effect / activation is negated, it can't be used again until Dasher leaves the GY. ●This effect can activate if the last thing to happen was a card being drawn by an effect (ex: Reload, Heard of the Underdog) during your Draw Phase. ●If you draw 2+ monsters simultaneously (by Reload) while 2 copies of Dasher are in your GY: you may activate both in a chain. ●VS. Drastic Drop Off: Your turn player priority allows Dasher's effect to be Chain Link 1. However, your opponent can chain Drastic Drop Off to discard the Monster Card you drew.

General rulings
1. You can only Tribute 1 monster to increase the ATK of "Destiny HERO - Dasher" during your Main Phases. 2. You can use the effect of "Destiny HERO - Dasher" to Special Summon 1 monster you draw only once while it is in the GY. If "Destiny HERO - Dasher" is returned to your hand or Deck and then sent back to the GY you could activate its effect again. 3. You can activate this effect when you draw a monster during your Draw Phase because of a card effect such as "Reload" or "Heart of the Underdog". 4. If you have 2 "Destiny HERO - Dasher" in your GY, and you activate "Reload" during your Draw Phase and draw 2 or more Monster Cards, you can activate the effects of both copies of "Destiny HERO - Dasher" in a chain. 5. "Destiny HERO - Dasher" cannot Tribute itself to activate its own effect. 6. If you have "Destiny HERO - Dasher" in your GY and your opponent has "Drastic Drop Off" Set, when you draw a Monster Card during your Draw Phase you can use priority to activate the effect of "Destiny HERO - Dasher". Your opponent can chain "Drastic Drop Off" to discard the Monster Card you drew. 7. If an effect is chained to the third effect of "Destiny HERO - Dasher" that removes it from the GY, the drawn monster is still Special Summoned.
