Dimensional Inversion

Trap · Normal

Banlist: Unlimited (3 copies)

Edison text

① When a monster(s) you own is banished by an opponent's card effect: Target 1 of those monsters; Special Summon that target.

Printed text (newer wording, may work differently)
Activate only when a monster(s) you own is removed from play by an opponent's effect. Special Summon 1 of those monsters.

Edison rulings

● You can activate this card if a monster(s) is banished by your opponent's "Macro Cosmos". If both players control "Macro Cosmos", the turn player's Macro is the one that banished the monster. ● This card's activation can have incorrect timing. ● You cannot target a Trap Monster with this card.

General rulings
1. If multiple monsters you control are being removed from play simultaneously, you can still activate "Dimensional Inversion". Select 1 of those monsters to be Special Summoned. 2. If your opponent controls "Macro Cosmos", "Banisher of the Light", etc., and an effect is activated by either player that sends a monster to the GY, you can activate "Dimensional Inversion". This is because your monster is being removed from play (instead of being sent to the GY) by the effect of your opponent's card. 3. If both players control "Macro Cosmos", etc., and a monster is sent to the GY, assume that the turn player's effect is the one that removes it from play. So in this case, the opponent can activate "Dimensional Inversion" but the turn player cannot. 4. You cannot activate "Dimensional Inversion" when a Trap Monster like "Embodiment of Apophis" is removed from play. 5. You can activate "Dimensional Inversion" even when a monster is removed from play temporarily. For example, if your monster is controlled by your opponent, and your opponent uses "Interdimensional Matter Transporter" on it, you can activate "Dimensional Inversion". 6. If a card is removed from play with "Lightforce Sword" or "Different Dimension Capsule", you cannot activate "Dimensional Inversion" because you cannot verify the card is a monster. 7. You cannot activate "Dimensional Inversion" during the Damage Step.
