Vampire Lord

Zombie / Effect · DARK · Level 5 · 2000 ATK / 1500 DEF

Banlist: Unlimited (3 copies)

Edison text

No Edison-Accurate PSCT available for this card.

Printed text (newer wording, may work differently)
If this card inflicts battle damage to your opponent: Declare 1 type of card (Monster, Spell, or Trap); your opponent sends 1 card of that type from their Deck to the GY. Once per turn, during your next Standby Phase after this card in your possession was destroyed and sent to your GY by an opponent's card effect: Special Summon this card from the GY.

Edison rulings

No Edison-specific rulings for this card.

General rulings
1. Your "Vampire Lord" will only be Special Summoned by his own effect when destroyed and sent to the GY by a card effect that your opponent controls, not by your own card effects or as a result of battle. 2. "Vampire Lord" will only return to play with his effect when the owner controls it, because "Vampire Lord" must be 'destroyed and sent to YOUR GY'. 3. When your opponent takes control of your "Vampire Lord", and it inflicts Battle Damage to your Life Points, then the first effect of "Vampire Lord" is activated. But the second effect will not be activated if "Vampire Lord" is destroyed and sent to the GY while your opponent controls him. 4. If you Summon "Vampire Lord" (except by its effect) and he is destroyed by your opponent's "Horn of Heaven" or "Solemn Judgment", "Vampire Lord" will be Special Summoned in your next Standby Phase. When it is Special Summoned by its own effect "Horn of Heaven" or "Solemn Judgment" cannot be activated, because it is being Summoned by its own Trigger Effect, not by a built-in 'this is how you Summon this monster' effect. 5. When "Vampire Lord" is destroyed (by a card effect) while in your hand, in your Deck, or on the field, he will be Special Summoned in your next Standby Phase. 6. When "Vampire Lord" is Tributed (including by your opponent with "Soul Exchange"), or when he is 'sent to the GY', he will not be Special Summoned by his own effect because he was not 'destroyed'. 7. If "Vampire Lord" is supposed to be Special Summoned, but your Standby Phase is skipped with "Solomon's Lawbook" or "Gamble", "Vampire Lord" won't be Special Summoned, not even on the following Standby Phase. 8. If "Vampire Lord" is supposed to be Special Summoned, but gets returned to your hand or Deck instead, he won't be Special Summoned. 9. If "Vampire Lord" is destroyed and sent to the GY by your opponent's card effect, but returns to the field that same turn and is then destroyed in a manner that would not allow him to be Special Summoned, then he is not Special Summoned. 10. You can keep Special Summoning "Vampire Lord" as long as each time he is destroyed by your opponent's card effect. 11. "Vampire Lord" is Special Summoned to your side of the field; you cannot Special Summon him to your opponent's side of the field. 12. When you have your "Vampire Lord" in play, Special Summoned by your "Premature Burial" or "Call of the Haunted", and your opponent destroys "Premature Burial" or "Call of the Haunted" with "Mystical Space Typhoon", the "Vampire Lord" won't be Special Summoned because it was destroyed by your card effect, not by your opponent's. 13. If your opponent controls your "Vampire Lord" with "Autonomous Action Unit", and "Autonomous Action Unit" is destroyed, "Vampire Lord" won't be Special Summoned because the owner did not control him. 14. When you Special Summon "Vampire Lord" with his effect, your opponent can negate the Special Summon with "Royal Oppression". Since the effect of "Vampire Lord" activates in the GY, "Vampire Lord" is never moved from the GY, so it is never destroyed. (A card already in the GY cannot be destroyed). Because it is not destroyed, its effect to Special Summon itself does not activate again. 15. If "Vampire Lord" is destroyed while equipped to "Relinquished", he won't be Special Summoned, no matter who controls him or who destroyed him. 16. If "Vampire Lord" is destroyed while face-down by "Sasuke Samurai", "Vampire Lord" will be Special Summoned by his effect. 17. "Vampire Lord" can be Special Summoned by his own effect even while "Necrovalley" is in play. 18. When "Vampire Lord" is Summoned while your opponent's "Rivalry of Warlords" is active and your only monsters are non-Zombie, "Vampire Lord" is destroyed, but is not considered destroyed by the effect of "Rivalry of Warlords" and won't be Special Summoned. 19. When your opponent doesn't have the type of card that you declared for "Vampire Lord", the effect of "Vampire Lord" disappears but you get to look at your opponent's Deck to confirm it. 20. It is the controller of "Vampire Lord", not necessarily the owner, that names a card type. 21. When you name 'Spell' for "Vampire Lord", it includes Normal, Quick-Play, Field, Ritual, Continuous, and Equip Spell Cards, and the same is true for Traps. 22. Card effects like "Penguin Knight", "Sword of Deep-Seated", and "Cockroach Knight" will activate their effects when sent to the GY by the effect of "Vampire Lord".
