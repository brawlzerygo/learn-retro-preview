Blackwing - Vayu the Emblem of Honor

Winged Beast / Effect / Tuner · DARK · Level 1 · 800 ATK / 0 DEF

Banlist: Unlimited (3 copies)

Edison text

① can't be used as Synchro Material. ② If this card is in your GY: You can target 1 non-Tuner "Blackwing" monster in your GY; banish both this card and the target, and if you do, Special Summon 1 "Blackwing” Synchro from your Extra Deck, whose Level equals the total Levels of those monsters (negate its effects).

Printed text (newer wording, may work differently)
This face-up card on the field can't be used as a Synchro Material. If this card is in the GY: You can target 1 non-Tuner "Blackwing" monster in your GY; banish both this card and that target, and if you do, Special Summon 1 "Blackwing" Synchro Monster from your Extra Deck whose Level equals the total Levels of the banished monsters, but its effects are negated.

In plain English

Tuner. From your GY, banish it and 1 non-Tuner Blackwing in your GY to Special Summon a Blackwing Synchro whose Level equals their total. That Synchro's effects are negated. It cannot be Synchro Material any other way.

Edison rulings

● This card’s ① effect is an effect, so it can be negated by effects (for example, by Shura). ● If “Royal Oppression” is chained to this card’s ② effect, this card is not banished, since the effect to banish both monsters is negated. This effect can then activate again that same turn. ● The monsters summoned this way are not Synchro Summoned monsters. This means that they can't be summoned by card effects if later sent to the GY/Banished (Goyo Guardian can't Summon a monster summoned this way). ● If this effect cannot fully resolve because you cannot Special Summon the Synchro Monster, Vayu and the target are still banished and the Synchro remains in the Extra Deck. E.G: - If a monster such as "Fossil Dyna Pachycephalo" is summoned in chain - If no appropriate Synchro monster exists in your Extra Deck on resolution - If "Rivalry of Walords" or "Gozen Match" is chained to this effect etc

General rulings
1. This effect targets 1 'Blackwing' monster in your GY. 2. Special Summoning a monster with this effect is not considered a Synchro Summon. 3. You can activate this effect even if "Discord" is active. 4. If the effect of "Blackwing - Vayu the Emblem of Honor" is being negated by "Skill Drain" you can use it for a Synchro Summon while it is on the field. 5. If the Synchro Monster you want to Summon with the effect of "Blackwing - Vayu the Emblem of Honor" is no longer in your Extra Deck when the effect resolves, you must Special Summon another appropriate Synchro Monster instead. If you do not have one, your opponent can check your Extra Deck to verify. 6. If an effect causes you to have no cards in your Extra Deck when the effect of "Blackwing - Vayu the Emblem of Honor" resolves, you still remove from play "Blackwing - Vayu the Emblem of Honor" and the selected 'Blackwing' monster. 7. If Vayu or the targeted 'Blackwing' monster isn't in the GY when this effect resolves, the effect is not applied.
