Blackwing - Sirocco the Dawn

Winged Beast / Effect · DARK · Level 5 · 2000 ATK / 900 DEF

Banlist: Unlimited (3 copies)

Edison text

Ⓢ If only your opponent controls a monster, you can Normal Summon/Set this card without Tributing. ① Once per turn: You can target 1 "Blackwing" monster you control; until the End Phase, it gains ATK equal to the total ATK of all other "Blackwing" monsters on the field. (You can't attack the turn you activate this effect, except with that target).

Printed text (newer wording, may work differently)
If your opponent controls a monster and you control no monsters, you can Normal Summon/Set this card without Tributing. Once per turn, during your Main Phase 1: You can target 1 "Blackwing" monster you control; until the end of this turn, it gains ATK equal to the total ATK of all "Blackwing" monsters currently on the field, except itself. Monsters other than the targeted monster can't attack during the turn you activate this effect.

Edison rulings

● The ① stops applying in the End Phase of the turn it was activated. ● The target gains ATK equal to the ATK of all Blackwings on the field - including your opponent's. ● If the target is set, and then flipped face-up, it doesn't gain any ATK, and you can't attack with it. ● This effect can target itself even if there are no other "Blackwing" monsters on the field, although the ATK will not be increased. ● This effect cannot be activated during your Main Phase 2. vs Negation: ● If the activation of this effect is negated (e.g. by "Gladiator Beast War Chariot"), you may attack with monsters other than the target. ● If the effect is negated (e.g. by "Forbidden Chalice"), you may not attack with monsters other than the target. ● If Sirocco's effects are negated after this effect has resolved (e.g. by "Skill Drain"), the ATK boost will remain and you still may not attack with other monsters.

General rulings
1. Your opponent's 'Blackwing' monsters are also counted. 2. The effect of "Blackwing - Sirocco the Dawn" targets the selected monster. 3. This monster can target itself, although the ATK increase will be zero if there are no other 'Blackwing' monsters on the field. 4. "Blackwing - Sirocco the Dawn" cannot activate its ATK boost in Main Phase 2. 5. The ATK boost lasts until the End Phase. 6. If you activate the effect of "Blackwing - Sirocco the Dawn", monsters Summoned afterwards cannot attack this turn. 7. If the targeted monster is no longer on the field when the effect resolves, all your other monsters still cannot attack this turn. 8. If the activation of the effect of "Blackwing - Sirocco the Dawn" is negated, then your other monsters may attack this turn. 9. If you have 2 copies of "Blackwing - Sirocco the Dawn" and target 2 different monsters with them, neither of those monsters can attack this turn. 10. If "Skill Drain" is activated after the effect of "Blackwing - Sirocco the Dawn" resolves, then the effect is not negated. The ATK boost remains, and the other monsters cannot attack this turn.
