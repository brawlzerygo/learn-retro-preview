Twin-Headed Behemoth

Dragon / Effect · WIND · Level 3 · 1500 ATK / 1200 DEF

Banlist: Unlimited (3 copies)

Edison text

No Edison-Accurate PSCT available for this card.

Printed text (newer wording, may work differently)
During the End Phase, if this card is in the GY because it was destroyed on the field and sent there this turn: You can Special Summon this card, but its ATK/DEF become 1000. You can only use this effect of "Twin-Headed Behemoth" once per Duel.

Edison rulings

●The effect of this card is only activated when it is destroyed. It won't activate if Tributed, discarded, or sent to the GY. ●When Twin-Headed Behemoth is Special Summoned by its own effect you can activate Card of Safe Return and draw 1 card. ●When Twin-Headed Behemoth is Special Summoned by its own effect and is equipped with Megamorph it is the original ATK/DEF that is doubled or halved. (ATK 3000 / DEF 2400 if doubled, ATK 750 / DEF 600 if halved) ●The opponent can't activate Bottomless Trap Hole when Twin-Headed Behemoth is Special Summoned by its own effect, because its ATK is already changed to 1000 at the time of Summoning. ●The condition that this card’s effect can only be used once per Duel can't be reset. So if it is Special Summoned by its own effect and later removed from play, flipped face-down, returned to your hand, destroyed and Special Summoned, or shuffled into your Deck, you STILL can't use its effect a second time. ●If removed from play temporarily by the effect of Dimensionhole or Interdimensional Matter Transporter, it will return with its original ATK/DEF, but its condition is not reset. ●If flipped face-down, and then Flip Summoned, it will have its original ATK/DEF, but its condition is not reset. ●If equipped to Relinquished and destroyed, you can use the effect of this card and it will be Special Summoned on your side of the field with ATK/DEF of 1000 points. (If you have not already used this effect once.) ●If this card is controlled by your opponent and is destroyed, it will be Special Summoned on your side of the field with ATK/DEF of 1000 points. (If you have not already used this effect once.) ●If Twin-Headed Behemoth would be Special Summoned by its own effect, but your Monster Card Zones are full, or the effect of Scapegoat prevents you from Summoning, it still considered that its effect was activated, and you can't use it again in the current Duel. ●You may still Special Summon Twin-Headed Behemoth with the effect of Monster Reborn, etc. after its effect has been used once. ●If you activate Monster Reborn to Special Summon Twin-Headed Behemoth in the turn it was destroyed, its effect has not yet been used, and it will activate the next time it is destroyed.

General rulings
1. The effect of this card is only activated when it is "destroyed". It will not activate if Tributed, discarded, or sent to the GY. 2. When "Twin-Headed Behemoth" is Special Summoned by its own effect you can activate "Card of Safe Return" and draw 1 card. 3. When "Twin-Headed Behemoth" is Special Summoned by its own effect and is equipped with "Megamorph" it is the original ATK/DEF that is doubled or halved. (ATK 3000 / DEF 2400 if doubled, ATK 750 / DEF 600 if halved) 4. The opponent cannot activate "Bottomless Trap Hole" when "Twin-Headed Behemoth" is Special Summoned by its own effect, because its ATK is already changed to 1000 at the time of Summoning. 5. The condition that this card's effect can only be used once per Duel cannot be reset. So if it is Special Summoned by its own effect and later removed from play, flipped face-down, returned to your hand, destroyed and Special Summoned, or shuffled into your Deck, you STILL cannot use its effect a second time. 6. If removed from play temporarily by the effect of "Dimensionhole" or "Interdimensional Matter Transporter", it will return with its original ATK/DEF, but its condition is not reset. 7. If flipped face-down, and then Flip Summoned, it will have its original ATK/DEF, but its condition is not reset. 8. If equipped to "Relinquished" and destroyed, you can use the effect of this card and it will be Special Summoned on your side of the field with ATK/DEF of 1000 points. (If you have not already used this effect once.) 9. If this card is controlled by your opponent and is destroyed, it will be Special Summoned on your side of the field with ATK/DEF of 1000 points. (If you have not already used this effect once.) 10. If "Twin-Headed Behemoth" would be Special Summoned by its own effect, but your Monster Card Zones are full, or the effect of "Scapegoat" prevents you from Summoning, it still considered that its effect was activated, and you cannot use it again in the current Duel. 11. You may still Special Summon "Twin-Headed Behemoth" with the effect of "Monster Reborn", etc. after its effect has been used once. 12. If you activate "Monster Reborn" to Special Summon "Twin-Headed Behemoth" in the turn it was destroyed, its effect has not yet been used, and it will activate the next time it is destroyed.
