Taking monsters

Brain Control

(67%, Limited): pay 800 LP and take one of your opponent's face-up monsters until the End Phase.

Edison text

Modern Brain Control can only take monsters that can be Normal Summoned. In Edison, it can take any face-up monster, Synchros included. See the errata list.

What you do with the borrowed monster matters most. You can attack with it. You can also use it as Synchro Material or Tribute it. Either way it goes to your opponent's Graveyard, because cards go to their owner's Graveyard. They do not get it back.

Mind Control

(31%, Limited) also takes a monster until the End Phase. It costs no LP and can take a face-down monster too. But the monster cannot attack or be Tributed.

Synchro Material is not a Tribute. So a face-up monster taken this way can still become part of your Synchro.

Think of it like this

Brain Control and Mind Control are a borrowed ingredient. You take your opponent's monster, put it in your recipe, and it never goes home.

Removing monsters

Book of Moon

(44%) is a Quick-Play that flips a face-up monster face-down. It is unlimited. Some uses:

  • Stop an attack, or stop a monster's effect that needs it face-up.
  • Flip your own Ryko or Fossil Dyna face-down, so you can flip it again on a later turn.
  • Clear effects that stay on a monster. A Plaguespreader flipped face-down loses its "banish it if it leaves" rule.

Nobleman of Crossout

(25%) destroys and banishes one Set monster. If it was a Flip monster, each player also banishes every copy of it from their Main Deck. That is strong against Ryko, Fossil Dyna and Super-Nimble Mega Hamster.

Less common removal

Common beginner mistakes
  • Playing Heavy Storm when you have more Set cards than your opponent.
  • Thinking a Brain Controlled Synchro goes to your Graveyard. It goes to its owner's.
  • Playing Allure of Darkness with no DARK in hand. You must send your whole hand to the Graveyard.

Recap

  • MST, Heavy Storm and Giant Trunade clear Set cards before you commit.
  • Brain Control can take any face-up monster in Edison, Synchros included.
  • A borrowed monster used as material goes to its owner's Graveyard for good.
  • Book of Moon and Nobleman handle monsters without a fight.
  • Allure, Reinforcement and Pot of Avarice keep your hand full.
