The plan is steady pressure. Each Gadget you summon puts the next one in your hand, so you never run out of monsters. Two Gadgets in hand are Level 4 + 4 = 8, enough to discard for Machina Fortress (2500 ATK). Fortress can come back from the GY the same way. When it is destroyed by battle, it destroys a card your opponent controls. Because the GY holds only Machines, Solidarity keeps giving +800 ATK.

Against it: the Gadget searches are triggers on a Normal Summon, so negates them and destroys the Gadget. stops all adding from the Deck except by drawing. Also watch : it can use Machines on either field. With , you can fuse their Machines away.

Diva Zombie: the Graveyard is a second hand

At a glance: Diva Zombie

Combo · Lists: Diva Zombie, TeleDAD · Lesson 26

How to spot it

Zombies that keep coming back: searching, and reviving, returning to the field.

What it wants

The Graveyard as a second hand: revive Zombies over and over, make Level 6 Zombie Synchros, and Tribute for Caius.

Strengths

  • Hard to run out of monsters: they come back from the GY.
  • revives a Zombie and banishes a monster from your GY.
  • negates the effects of monsters their Zombies destroy by battle, even in the GY.

Weaknesses

  • It needs the Graveyard: and take its plays away.
  • negates DARK effects on the field and in the GY.

Dragon Turbo: draw, discard, bring them back

At a glance: Dragon Turbo

Combo · Lists: Dragon Turbo, Dragon Aggro · Lesson 26

How to spot it

Draw Spells that discard Dragons: , and , then reviving a .

What it wants

Draw hard, fill the GY with big Dragons, then bring them back with Red-Eyes Darkness Metal Dragon, several times in one turn.

Strengths

  • It draws two cards at a time, turn after turn: Trade-In, Cards of Consonance and Super Rejuvenation.
  • 3000 ATK Blue-Eyes on the field fast.
  • turns a discard into a Blue-Eyes search.

Weaknesses

  • Red-Eyes and Blue-Eyes Special Summon: , and stop them.
  • stops White Stone's search.
  • banishes a Blue-Eyes before Red-Eyes revives it.

What to expect

  • Several Red-Eyes Darkness Metal Dragons in one turn.
  • sending five Dragons to the GY.
  • reviving White Stone to give Red-Eyes a Dragon to banish.

How to beat it

  • Cut off the Special Summons and the draws do nothing.
  • Save D.D. Crow for the Blue-Eyes they mean to revive.

Don't

  • Letting a turn of draws go unanswered: the next turn is the big one.
Good against: Gladiator Beasts
Even with: Vayu Turbo, Quickdraw Plants, Lightsworn, Frog Monarchs, Machina Gadget, Diva Zombie

The pattern

Find what each engine needs. Gadgets need summon triggers and searches. Zombies need the Graveyard. Dragons need Special Summons. Take that away and the deck slows down.

Recap

  • Each Gadget searches the next one. Two Gadgets in hand summon Machina Fortress.
  • Diva Zombie revives Zombies over and over and makes Plaguespreader Synchros.
  • Dragon Turbo draws by discarding Dragons, then Red-Eyes Darkness Metal Dragon brings them back.
  • Answer each deck by cutting off what its engine needs.
