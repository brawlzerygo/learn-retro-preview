The Big Synchros, Part 1

Levels 4 to 6: the Synchros you make with small monsters. 5 min · 6 flashcards

Why these cards matter

In Lesson 5 you met the Extra Deck "toolbox." Now you learn what each tool does. This lesson covers the smaller ones, Levels 4 to 6. Lesson 12 covers Levels 7 to 9.

Small Synchros matter because they are easy to make. A Level 2 Tuner plus a Level 3 or 4 monster is enough. You will see them in game after game.

The numbers below come from the 55 decks on this site. They count a card if a deck runs it anywhere: Main, Extra or Side Deck.

Level 4 and 5: Armory Arm, Catastor, Magical Android

Armory Arm

is a Synchro that can become an Equip Card. Once per turn, you pick one of two things:

  • Equip it to a monster on the field. That monster gains 1000 ATK.
  • Unequip it and Special Summon it in Attack Position.

While it is equipped, it has one more trick. If the equipped monster destroys a monster in battle and sends it to the Graveyard, your opponent takes damage equal to that monster's ATK.

It can even go on your opponent's monster. If that monster then destroys yours in battle, your opponent takes the damage. Armory Arm is still your card.

Ally of Justice Catastor

is in 87% of the decks on this site. Its effect is simple. At the start of the Damage Step, if it battles a monster that is not DARK, that monster is destroyed. ATK does not matter.

It works when Catastor attacks and when it is attacked. It does nothing against DARK monsters, so those just battle it normally.

The face-down trap

The start of the Damage Step happens before a Set monster is flipped face-up. So if Catastor attacks a face-down monster, its effect does not activate. It only sees face-up monsters.

Magical Android

has 2400 ATK, the highest of the Level 5s here. During your End Phase, you gain 600 LP for each Psychic monster you control. Android is a Psychic itself, so it always gives you at least 600.

Catastor and Android are both Level 5. Catastor is the answer to a strong non-DARK monster. Android is the bigger body when there is nothing special to answer.

Level 6: Goyo Guardian and Brionac

Goyo Guardian

has 2800 ATK. When it destroys a monster in battle and sends it to the Graveyard, you can Special Summon that monster to your field in Defense Position.

So every battle it wins takes a monster away from your opponent and gives it to you. That new monster can then be Synchro Material for your next play.

Edison text

Modern Goyo needs an EARTH Tuner. In Edison, any Tuner works. It is Limited, so you get one copy. See Lesson 10 and the errata list.

The effect needs the monster to go to the Graveyard, and Goyo must still be face-up when it triggers. If the destroyed monster is banished instead, nothing happens.

Brionac, Dragon of the Ice Barrier

is in 89% of the decks here. Discard any number of cards, then target that many cards on the field. They go back to the hand.

In Edison, you can use it as many times per turn as you can pay. You can target cards on either field. That makes it flexible:

  • Bounce your opponent's Set card before you attack.
  • Bounce an opponent's Synchro. It goes back to their Extra Deck, not their hand.
  • Bounce your own card to save it or reuse it.
Think of it like this

Brionac turns cards in your hand into removal. Each card you discard is one card you take off the field. You choose how much to spend.

Brionac is Limited. The discard is a cost, so it happens even if the effect is stopped.

One more you will see

is a Level 6 in 22% of the decks. It deals piercing damage, and it gains 300 ATK each time it deals battle damage to your opponent.

Common beginner mistakes
  • Using Catastor's effect on a face-down monster. It does not trigger.
  • Using Brionac only once per turn because of the modern text.
  • Thinking a bounced Synchro goes to the hand. It goes to the Extra Deck.

Recap

  • Armory Arm equips for +1000 ATK and burns for the ATK of what it destroys.
  • Catastor destroys any face-up non-DARK monster it battles.
  • Magical Android is a 2400 body that gains LP.
  • Goyo Guardian takes the monsters it destroys in battle. Any Tuner works in Edison.
  • Brionac bounces one card per discard, as often as you like.
