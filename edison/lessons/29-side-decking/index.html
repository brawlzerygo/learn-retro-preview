Side Decking

What to swap in between games, using the cards real lists side. 4 min · 6 flashcards

What the Side Deck is for

A match is several games against the same opponent. After game 1, you know what deck they play. Your Side Deck holds up to 15 cards you can swap into your Main Deck between games. You take a card out for every card you put in.

Think of it like this

A football team watches the first half, then changes its plan at halftime. The players on the bench are your Side Deck.

Your Main Deck is built to do well against everything. Your Side Deck holds cards that are great against one deck and weak against the rest.

How to side, step by step

  1. Name their engine. What does their deck need most? The Graveyard, Special Summons, Tributes or searches? Lesson 26 shows how to find it.
  2. Pick your side cards for it. Use the table above. Bring in only cards that hit what their deck needs.
  3. Take out your weakest cards for this matchup. A card that does nothing against them is the first to go. For example, Mask of Restrict is weak against a deck that never Tributes.
  4. Check your own deck still works. Fossil Dyna and Royal Oppression stop your Synchros too. Don't side them in if your own plan needs Special Summons.
  5. Put it back after the match. Return to your main 40 before you face the next opponent.
A real example

The Machina Gadget Side Deck has 2 . It lets each player control only one Type of monster. Every monster in Machina Gadget is a Machine, so it barely hurts them. A deck that mixes Types loses monsters.

Common mistake

Siding in too many cards. If you change half your deck, your engine stops working. A few strong swaps beat many small ones.

Two creators made full guides on this. Watch E3 Yu-Gi-Oh's "How To Sideboard in Edison Format" and IMNerd (Frazier Smith)'s "Side Decking 101" in the Library.

Recap

  • Between games, swap Side Deck cards in, one out for each one in.
  • D.D. Crow, Cyber Dragon and Dust Tornado are the most common side cards in our lists.
  • Side for what their engine needs, and take out what does nothing against them.
  • Check that your side cards don't stop your own deck.
