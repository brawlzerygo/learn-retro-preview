The Big Synchros, Part 2

Levels 7 to 9: the Synchros that end turns and win games. 5 min · 6 flashcards

Level 7: Black Rose and Ancient Fairy

Black Rose Dragon

You met in Lesson 4. When it is Synchro Summoned, you can destroy every card on the field. Yours too. It is Limited, and it is in 89% of the decks here.

The effect is optional. You use it when your opponent has more on the field than you do. If you are ahead, you can skip it and just keep a 2400 ATK monster.

It has a second effect: banish a Plant from your Graveyard to turn one of your opponent's Defense Position monsters to Attack Position with 0 ATK until the End Phase. Plant decks use this. Most decks only care about the first effect.

Ancient Fairy Dragon

has 2100 ATK and 3000 DEF. Once per turn, it can Special Summon a Level 4 or lower monster from your hand. You cannot conduct your Battle Phase that turn.

That extra monster can be Synchro Material. Its other effect destroys a Field Spell, gains you 1000 LP, and lets you add a Field Spell from your Deck. It is in 42% of the decks.

Level 8: the big four

Stardust Dragon

is in every one of the 55 decks on this site. It is a shield for your whole field.

  1. Your opponent activates a card or effect that would destroy a card on the field.
  2. You Tribute Stardust Dragon. That activation is negated and destroyed.
  3. In the End Phase, Stardust comes back from your Graveyard.

It stops cards like Black Rose Dragon, Heavy Storm, Mirror Force, Bottomless Trap Hole and Dark Armed Dragon's effect. It only works on things that would surely destroy a card.

The big idea

Stardust Dragon does not stop removal that does not destroy. Bouncing, banishing without destroying, or sending to the Graveyard all get past it.

Thought Ruler Archfiend

has 2700 ATK. When it destroys a monster in battle and sends it to the Graveyard, you gain LP equal to that monster's original ATK.

It also protects itself. It is a Psychic. When a Spell or Trap targets exactly one Psychic, you can pay 1000 LP to negate it. That covers cards like Book of Moon, Brain Control and Dimensional Prison aimed at it. It only negates cards, not monster effects.

Colossal Fighter

starts at 2800 ATK. It gains 100 ATK for each Warrior in both Graveyards. When it is destroyed in battle, you can Special Summon a Warrior from either Graveyard. It can even bring back itself.

Red Dragon Archfiend

has 3000 ATK. When it attacks a Defense Position monster, after damage, all of your opponent's Defense Position monsters are destroyed.

The cost: in your End Phase, your other monsters that did not attack this turn are destroyed. Plan around that before you summon it.

One more Level 8

is in 51% of the decks. It needs non-Tuner DARK monsters as material. Once per turn, it loses 500 ATK and DEF to send one of your opponent's monsters to the Graveyard. That is not destruction, so Stardust Dragon cannot stop it.

Level 9: Mist Wurm

needs a Tuner plus two or more non-Tuners. When it is Synchro Summoned, it returns 1 to 3 of your opponent's cards to the hand. This effect must be used, and it targets.

Three cards at once can clear Set Traps right before you attack. Bounced Synchros go back to the Extra Deck.

Picking the right one

Most Extra Decks hold several of these. Pick by the problem in front of you.

ProblemCardDescription
They have more cards outWipe the whole field, then rebuild first.
You fear removalStops one destroy effect, then returns.
A wall of defendersOne attack on a defender clears all of them.
Set cards in the wayBounces up to three cards on summon.
One strong monsterSends it to the Graveyard, past Stardust.
You need a big attacker2800 or more, and it brings back a Warrior.
Common beginner mistakes
  • Using Stardust against a bounce or a banish that does not destroy. It cannot.
  • Summoning Red Dragon Archfiend and forgetting your other monsters. Those that did not attack are destroyed.
  • Trying to make Mist Wurm with one non-Tuner. It needs two or more.

Recap

  • Black Rose Dragon can wipe the field when Synchro Summoned.
  • Stardust Dragon stops one destroy effect and comes back in the End Phase. It is in every deck on this site.
  • Thought Ruler, Colossal Fighter and Red Dragon Archfiend are big attackers with extra jobs.
  • Dark End Dragon sends to the Graveyard, which Stardust cannot stop.
  • Mist Wurm bounces up to three cards and needs two non-Tuners.
