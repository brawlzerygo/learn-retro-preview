Battle and Life Points

How attacks work, and how damage is worked out. 8 min · 6 flashcards

Already know how to play? This part is optional.Skip to Edison 101 →

Declaring an attack

In your Battle Phase, pick one of your face-up Attack Position monsters and pick a target: one of your opponent's monsters. Each monster can attack once per turn.

If your opponent controls no monsters, you can attack them directly. They lose LP equal to your monster's ATK.

The battle math

Your attacking monster's ATK is compared with the target. Which number you compare against depends on the target's position.

Attacking a monster in Attack Position: ATK against ATK

SituationWhat happens
Your ATK is higherTheir monster is destroyed. They lose the difference in LP.
Your ATK is lowerYour monster is destroyed. You lose the difference.
They are equalBoth monsters are destroyed. Nobody loses LP.

Example: (2000 ATK) attacks (1000 ATK) in Attack Position. Sangan is destroyed. Your opponent loses 2000 minus 1000 = 1000 LP.

Attacking a monster in Defense Position: ATK against DEF

SituationWhat happens
Your ATK is higherTheir monster is destroyed. Nobody loses LP.
Your ATK is lowerNothing is destroyed. You lose the difference.
They are equalNothing happens.

Example: Warwolf (2000 ATK) attacks Sangan in Defense Position (600 DEF). Sangan is destroyed, but your opponent loses no LP.

Think of it like this

A monster in Defense Position is behind a shield. You can break the shield, but the hit does not reach the player behind it.

Attacking a face-down monster

When you attack a Set monster, it is flipped face-up first. Then the battle is worked out with its DEF. If it has a FLIP effect, that effect happens.

You do not know what you are hitting. A face-down cannot be destroyed in battle, and it costs you 1000 LP when you attack it face-down.

Life Points

  • LP go down from battle damage and from card effects ("deal 500 damage").
  • Some cards also make you pay LP as a cost, like Solemn Judgment.
  • Some cards let you gain LP.
  • When a player's LP reach 0, they lose right away.
Common beginner mistakes
  • Taking damage when your monster destroys a Defense Position monster. Nobody takes damage then.
  • Attacking directly while your opponent still has a monster, even a face-down one.
  • Attacking twice with the same monster.

Recap

  • Each Attack Position monster can attack once per turn.
  • ATK against ATK: the loser is destroyed, and its controller takes the difference.
  • ATK against DEF: no damage to the defender. A wall that is too strong hurts the attacker.
  • No monsters to protect them? Attack directly.
