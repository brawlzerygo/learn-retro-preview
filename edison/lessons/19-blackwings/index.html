Blackwings

A flock of DARK birds that summon each other and win the battle. 6 min · 6 flashcards

At a glance: Blackwings

Aggro · Lists: Blackwing, Hybrid · Lesson 19

How to spot it

A Blackwing Normal Summoned with face-up, or Special Summoning itself next to another bird. DARK Winged Beasts with 1700 to 2000 ATK and one or two Set cards.

What it wants

Two or three attackers in one turn, a won battle every turn, and spare birds turned into Level 6 to 8 Synchros.

The deck

Blackwings are DARK Winged Beasts. They are listed in the Best tier on this site. Their plan is simple: put two or three attackers on the field in one turn, win every battle, and turn spare birds into Synchros. Open the Blackwing list to follow along.

Here is a common turn. It uses only the cards' own text.

  1. With Black Whirlwind face-up, Normal Summon . Whirlwind adds Bora (1700 is less than 1800).
  2. You control a Blackwing, so Special Summon from your hand. It does not start a Chain.
  3. Attack. If Shura destroys a monster and sends it to the GY, Special Summon a Blackwing with 1500 or less ATK from your Deck, like Gale. Its effects are negated, but it can still be Synchro Material.
  4. In Main Phase 2, Synchro Summon with Gale (3) and Bora (4): a Level 7, like or Black Rose Dragon.
Ordering two triggers

A ruling for : when you Normal Summon it with Black Whirlwind face-up, both effects trigger. Put Blizzard as Chain Link 1 and Whirlwind as Chain Link 2. Now your opponent can only respond to Whirlwind, so cards like Pulling the Rug and Royal Oppression cannot be used on Blizzard's revive.

Hybrid: Blackwings with Dark Grepher

The Hybrid list, also in the Best tier, is a Blackwing variant. It keeps the birds and Black Whirlwind, but adds 3 Dark Grepher, 3 Vayu, Burial from a Different Dimension, 2 Royal Oppression and 2 Dark Bribe. Dark Grepher can discard a DARK to send another DARK from the Deck to the GY, like Vayu or Sirocco. That sets up Vayu faster. Burial returns banished monsters to the GY so Vayu can be used again. Lesson 22 covers this idea in full.

Weaknesses and how to play against it

These all follow from the cards' text:

  • Everything is DARK. Consecrated Light stops both players from summoning DARKs or attacking with them.
  • Bora, Gale and Vayu Special Summon. , and Vanity's Fiend shut them off. Blizzard can't be Special Summoned at all.
  • The engine starts with a Normal Summon. Pulling the Rug negates Blizzard's revive.
  • Vayu and Dark Armed Dragon banish from the GY. Kycoo the Ghost Destroyer says your opponent can't banish cards from either GY.
  • It must attack to win. Dimensional Prison and Mirror Force hit it hard. Destroy Black Whirlwind with Mystical Space Typhoon or Dust Tornado to slow the searches.
Common mistakes against Blackwings
  • Forgetting Kalut. Assume a battling Blackwing can gain 1400 ATK while they have cards in hand.
  • Setting a weak monster in front of Bora or Armed Wing. Both pierce.
  • Counting their field only. Shura can pull a new bird from the Deck mid-battle.

Want to see it played? The Library has Blackwing guides by E3 Yu-Gi-Oh and IMNerd (Frazier Smith).

Recap

  • Black Whirlwind turns each Normal Summon into a search for a lower-ATK bird.
  • Bora and Gale Special Summon themselves next to another Blackwing.
  • Blizzard and Gale are the Tuners for Armed Wing, Armor Master and Silverwind. Vayu gives a second route.
  • Fight them with DARK locks, Special Summon locks, Pulling the Rug and battle Traps.
