The Banlist

What is gone, what is limited, and what that tells you about the format. 8 min · 5 flashcards

The idea

Konami keeps a banlist that limits how many copies of the strongest cards you can play. Edison uses the official banlist from March 2010. It has three tiers:

StatusCopies allowedCards on the list
F Forbidden043
L Limited170
S Semi-Limited219

The count covers your Main, Extra, and Side Deck together. Every other card in the pool is allowed at 3.

Famous cards that are gone

Many of the most famous cards in the game are Forbidden here. They were too strong, so you can't play them at all:

  • Draw and search: Pot of Greed, Graceful Charity, Painful Choice, Delinquent Duo
  • Removal: Raigeki, Dark Hole, Harpie's Feather Duster, Ring of Destruction
  • Steal and revive: Snatch Steal, Change of Heart, Monster Reborn, Premature Burial, Confiscation
  • Monsters: Black Luster Soldier, Chaos Emperor Dragon, Dark Magician of Chaos, Witch of the Black Forest, Magician of Faith, Tsukuyomi, Sinister Serpent, Yata-Garasu
  • Traps: Imperial Order, Crush Card Virus

Think of it like this

The banlist is a map of what scared players in 2010. A Limited card is a card everyone wanted to play. When you see one, slow down and read it.

Recap

  • Edison uses the March 2010 list: 43 Forbidden, 70 Limited, 19 Semi-Limited.
  • Famous power cards like Pot of Greed, Graceful Charity, Raigeki, Dark Hole, Monster Reborn and Snatch Steal are Forbidden.
  • Many staples, like Heavy Storm and Mystical Space Typhoon, are Limited.
  • Black Rose, Brionac, and Goyo are Limited. Royal Oppression and Dandylion are at 2.
  • Pot of Avarice, Book of Moon, and Dimensional Prison are at 3.
