Chains and Priority

What happens when both players respond to each other. 9 min · 6 flashcards

Already know how to play? This part is optional.Skip to Edison 101 →

The idea

When you activate a card, your opponent gets a chance to respond before it happens. Then you get a chance to respond to their response. Cards stack up like this into a Chain.

  • The first card is Chain Link 1.
  • A response to it is Chain Link 2, and so on.
  • When nobody wants to add anything, the Chain resolves backwards: the last card added happens first.
Think of it like this

A stack of plates. Each new card goes on top. When you clear the stack, you take the top plate first.

An example

It is your turn. Gene-Warped Warwolf attacks. Your opponent has set. You hold , a Quick-Play Spell.

OpponentYour monster attacks. They activate Mirror Force (Chain Link 1).
YouYou respond with Book of Moon on your own Warwolf (Chain Link 2).
Link 2Book of Moon resolves first. Warwolf turns face-down in Defense Position.
Link 1Mirror Force resolves. It destroys Attack Position monsters only, so Warwolf survives.
Destroying a card does not stop it

Once a Normal Spell or Trap is activated, it resolves even if it gets destroyed on the Chain. Chaining to Mirror Force does not save your monsters. Only a card that negates, like , stops it. Continuous Spells and Traps are different: they do need to stay on the field to work.

Spell Speed: who can respond to what

Every card and effect has a Spell Speed from 1 to 3. To respond on a Chain, your card must be Spell Speed 2 or higher, and at least as fast as the card it responds to.

Spell SpeedCardsCan it respond?
1Normal, Equip, Continuous, Field and Ritual Spells. Ignition, Trigger and Flip effects.No. It can only start a Chain.
2Quick-Play Spells, Normal and Continuous Traps, Quick EffectsYes, to Speed 1 or 2
3Counter TrapsYes, to anything. Only another Counter Trap can respond to it.

Example: your opponent activates (Speed 1). You can chain a set (Speed 3) to negate it. Or you can chain a set Trap like (Speed 2) to use it before Heavy Storm destroys it. You cannot chain Mirror Force, because nothing is attacking.

Priority: who acts first

The turn player, the one whose turn it is, always gets the first chance to act in each phase. After they do something, the opponent gets a chance to respond. Then it goes back and forth.

In Edison there is one important extra: after you summon a monster, you can use its Ignition Effect before your opponent responds to the summon. Lesson 10 covers this in detail, along with "missing the timing" and the Damage Step.

Common beginner mistakes
  • Resolving a Chain from the first link. Always start from the last one.
  • Trying to respond with a Normal Spell. Speed 1 cards cannot respond.
  • Answering a Counter Trap with a normal Trap. Only a Counter Trap can respond to a Counter Trap.

Recap

  • Responses stack up into a Chain.
  • A Chain resolves backwards: last in, first out.
  • Speed 1 starts Chains. Speed 2 and 3 can respond. Counter Traps are Speed 3.
  • The turn player acts first. You are ready for Edison 101.
