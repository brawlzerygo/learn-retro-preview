What stops a Synchro Summon

A Synchro Summon is a Special Summon. So cards that stop Special Summons stop Synchros. Some cards stop the Summon itself. Others only answer the monster once it is out.

Edison rulings
  • Rai-Oh can negate a Synchro Summon or summoning itself. It can't negate a monster summoned by a card effect, like .
  • Solemn Judgment can't negate a monster effect that starts a Chain, such as summoning itself.
Watch your own cards

Royal Oppression, Fossil Dyna and Vanity's Fiend stop both players. If you play them, your own Synchros stop too.

Edison rulings for Royal Oppression
  • It can't be used in the Damage Step.
  • It can negate cards that could Special Summon, like .
  • It can negate a Gladiator Beast tag out. The monster stays in the Deck.

Edison rulings
  • Road must be chained right to the card that would destroy 2 or more of your cards, and it must be certain they would be destroyed.
  • A Stardust Dragon summoned by Road was not Synchro Summoned. If it is later sent to the GY or banished, it can't be summoned again.
  • Stardust can only negate effects that would surely destroy a card.

Recap

  • Solemn, Royal Oppression, Rai-Oh, Fossil Dyna and Vanity's Fiend stop a Synchro Summon. Mask of Restrict doesn't.
  • Destroy Royal Oppression with MST or Dust Tornado, or negate it with Royal Decree or Trap Stun.
  • Starlight Road needs 2 or more of your cards to be destroyed.
  • Stardust stops destruction, not bouncing or banishing.
