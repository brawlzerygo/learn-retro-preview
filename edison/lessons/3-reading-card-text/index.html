Reading Card Text

Destroy, send, banish, return, target, once per turn, cost, and "you can." 8 min · 8 flashcards

Why the words matter

Yu-Gi-Oh! cards use the same few words again and again, and each one means something exact. Two cards can look alike and work very differently because of one word. Learn these words once, and every card gets easier to read.

Destroy vs send to the Graveyard

Destroyed means a card was destroyed by battle or by an effect that says "destroy." It then goes to the Graveyard.

Sent to the Graveyard covers every way a card gets there: destroyed, discarded, Tributed, or used as Synchro Material.

So every destroyed card is sent to the Graveyard, but not every card sent to the Graveyard was destroyed.

Banish vs return to the hand

Banish puts a card out of play. It stays there for the rest of the duel unless an effect brings it back. Cards printed in 2010 say "remove from play". That means exactly the same thing.

  • destroys a summoned monster with 1500 or more ATK, then banishes it.
  • banishes a monster when it declares an attack.

Return to the hand sends a card back to its owner's hand. It isn't destroyed, and it can be played again. does this. A Synchro or Fusion Monster can't go to the hand, so it goes back to the Extra Deck instead.

Think of it like this

The Graveyard is a bench next to the field. Banished cards have left the building. A card returned to the hand is back in the locker room, ready to play again.

Target

A card targets when you pick the card it affects as you activate it. targets: you choose 1 Spell or Trap when you play it. doesn't target: it destroys every Spell and Trap on the field.

If the target is gone

If the card you targeted is no longer there when the effect resolves, the effect does nothing.

Once per turn

"Once per turn" means just that: you can use the effect one time each turn. can send cards from the top of your Deck to the Graveyard once per turn.

If an effect does not say "once per turn," you can use it as many times as you can pay for it. In Edison, has no such limit, so you can use it again and again, as long as you have cards to discard.

Cost

A cost is what you pay to activate a card: Life Points, a discard, or a Tribute. You pay it first, and you don't get it back, even if the card is stopped. costs 800 LP. If your opponent negates it, the 800 LP stay paid.

"You can" means you choose

If an effect says "you can," it is optional: you decide whether to use it. If it doesn't, the effect is mandatory: it happens whether you want it or not.

Common beginner mistakes
  • Drawing with Card Trooper after using it as Synchro Material. It was not destroyed.
  • Putting a returned Synchro in your hand. It goes to the Extra Deck.
  • Asking for your LP back when your Brain Control is negated.
  • Skipping the search from Sangan. It is mandatory.

Recap

  • Destroyed cards are sent to the Graveyard, but many cards are sent without being destroyed.
  • Banish and "remove from play" are the same thing.
  • A targeted effect does nothing if its target is gone.
  • No "once per turn" means you can use it as often as you can pay.
  • Costs are paid first and never refunded.
  • "You can" is optional. Without it, the effect must happen.
