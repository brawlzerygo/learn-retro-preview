Your First Game

Where to play, a simple deck to start with, and what to watch for. 7 min · 4 flashcards

Where to play

  • DuelingBook and EDOPro are free online simulators. Both support the Edison banlist.
  • Format Library runs Edison tournaments and posts decklists.
  • edisonformat.com has rules, decklists, and events.

A simple first deck: Machina Gadgets

Many players suggest Machina Gadgets for beginners. The deck is simple, and it is cheap to build in real life.

The engine is three monsters that find each other:

  • adds Red Gadget when summoned.
  • adds Yellow Gadget when summoned.
  • adds Green Gadget when summoned.
Think of it like this

Each Gadget hands you the next one, like a relay race. Every summon replaces itself, so your hand never runs dry.

You fill the rest of the deck with the staples from Lesson 7. You will learn the deck's full plan later. For now, it lets you focus on the game instead of on combos.

Your first 10 games: a checklist

  1. Count Levels every turn. Look at both fields. Ask: "What Synchros can they make?"
  2. Count set cards before you commit. Lesson 7 is your guide.
  3. Say your plays out loud. It slows you down and catches mistakes.
  4. Read every card you do not know. Tap it, and check the Glossary.
  5. After each game, write down one mistake. Only one. Fix that next game.

How you won

Mirror Force punished overextending. You cleared Bottomless before your big summon. Book of Moon stopped a Synchro. A bait Synchro drew out Solemn Judgment, and its cost put them in range. Brionac cleared the way for the final attack.

Practice without an opponent

Use the Board on this site. Build any hand or field, then practice finding every Synchro you can make. It is the fastest way to train your eyes for Levels.

Recap

  • Play on DuelingBook or EDOPro with the Edison banlist.
  • Machina Gadgets is a simple, friendly first deck.
  • Every turn, count Levels and count set cards.
  • Fix one mistake per game.
