Common Tuners

The small monsters that make Synchros possible, and how each one reaches the field. 4 min · 6 flashcards

Tuners are personal

The big Synchros are the same in almost every deck. Tuners are not. Each deck picks Tuners that fit its own monsters.

Only one Tuner is a staple by this site's rule (in 25% or more of the decks): , in 35%. Every other Tuner shows up in fewer decks. And 11 of the 55 decks run no Tuner in the Main Deck at all.

Think of it like this

The Synchros are a shared toolbox. The Tuner is the key that opens it. Every deck cuts its own key.

When you meet a new Tuner, ask two questions:

  1. How does it get to the field? A Tuner that brings a second monster with it saves you a card.
  2. What is its Level? Add it to the monsters next to it. That tells you which Synchros are possible.

Plaguespreader Zombie

is a Level 2 DARK Zombie Tuner. It is Limited. Its effect works from the Graveyard:

  • Put 1 card from your hand on top of your Deck.
  • Special Summon Plaguespreader from your Graveyard.
  • If it leaves the field after that, it is banished.

So you can Normal Summon it and use it as Synchro Material. It goes to the Graveyard. Later you bring it back with its own effect and use it again. The second time, it is banished.

The effect is not once per turn. And because it does not use your Normal Summon, you can Normal Summon another monster in the same turn.

The Level math with common monsters

Most Tuners are Level 2 or 3. Here is what a Level 2 Tuner makes with monsters from Lesson 14:

Level 2 Tuner plusTotalDescription
(3)5Catastor or Magical Android
(4)6Goyo Guardian or Brionac
(5)7Black Rose Dragon or Ancient Fairy Dragon
(6)8Stardust Dragon and the other Level 8s

Add 1 to every total for a Level 3 Tuner. The habit to build: see a Tuner, add the Levels, name the Synchro.

Common beginner mistakes
  • Bringing back Plaguespreader and forgetting it is banished when it leaves the field.
  • Using Debris Dragon for a non-Dragon Synchro, or with a Level 4 partner.
  • Expecting a monster revived by Junk Synchron to use its effect. Its effects are negated.

Recap

  • Synchros are shared. Tuners change with each deck.
  • Plaguespreader Zombie is the one staple Tuner. It comes back from the Graveyard, then is banished when it leaves.
  • The best Tuners bring a second monster with them.
  • Debris Dragon and Vayu have rules attached. Read them.
  • See a Tuner, add the Levels, name the Synchro.
