Tokens

Small monsters made by card effects, and why they matter so much. 8 min · 7 flashcards

The idea

A token is a monster made by a card effect. There is no real card for it. Players use a coin, a die, or a token card to mark it on the field.

The effect that makes the token tells you everything about it: its Level, Attribute, type, ATK, and DEF.

Token rules
  • Tokens count as monsters on the field.
  • If a token leaves the field for any reason, it disappears. It never goes to the Graveyard or to a hand.
  • Tokens with a Level can be Synchro Material.

Scapegoat: a wall and an ingredient

is a Quick-Play Spell. It makes 4 Sheep Tokens in Defense Position, each Level 1 with 0 ATK and 0 DEF. The sheep are walls: they block attacks and buy you time.

Sheep cannot be Tributed for a Tribute Summon. But nothing stops them from being Synchro Material.

++=
Level 3 + 1 + 1 = Level 5

Your walls just became a 2200 ATK monster that destroys non-DARK monsters in battle. In Edison, Scapegoat is a wall and an ingredient at the same time.

Timing matters

Scapegoat says you cannot summon any other monster the turn you activate it, and a Synchro Summon counts. So the usual play is to activate Scapegoat on your opponent's turn, often when they attack. Then on your turn, Normal Summon a Tuner like and Synchro with the sheep.

Think of it like this

The sheep are sandbags. In Edison, they are sandbags you can melt down and turn into a cannon.

Dandylion: a deck built on tokens

is a Level 3 Plant. When it is sent to the Graveyard, it makes 2 s. Each is Level 1 with 0 ATK and 0 DEF.

The key word is "sent." It does not matter how Dandylion gets there. It works when Dandylion is:

  • Discarded from your hand
  • Sent from your Deck (milled)
  • Destroyed on the field
  • Used as Synchro Material

Watch what does with it. Quickdraw can Special Summon itself by sending 1 monster from your hand to the Graveyard.

  1. Send Dandylion from your hand to Special Summon Quickdraw Synchron (Level 5 Tuner).
  2. Dandylion hits the Graveyard. You get 2 Fluff Tokens.
  3. Synchro Summon with Quickdraw + 1 Fluff Token: 5 + 1 = 6.
  4. Summon . You still have 1 Fluff Token, and your Normal Summon is still unused.
+=
Level 5 + 1 = Level 6

One card from your hand became a Level 6 Synchro and a token. This is the heart of Quickdraw Plants. According to edisonformat.com, a Quickdraw deck (Quickdraw Dandywarrior) won the April 2010 tournament in Edison that the format is named after. You will study it in Lesson 20.

Quickdraw's limit

Quickdraw Synchron can only be used for Synchros that need a "Synchron" Tuner, like Drill Warrior or Junk Warrior. It cannot make Stardust Dragon or Black Rose Dragon.

Common beginner mistakes
  • Thinking sheep can never be used for anything. They can be Synchro Material.
  • Trying to Tribute Summon with Fluff Tokens the turn they appear. That is not allowed.
  • Forgetting Dandylion works from anywhere it is sent from, even your Deck.

Recap

  • Tokens are monsters without a card. They vanish when they leave the field.
  • Tokens with a Level can be Synchro Material.
  • Scapegoat is a wall and an ingredient.
  • Dandylion makes 2 tokens whenever it is sent to the Graveyard.
