The Extra Deck

Your second pile of cards: what goes in it and how it works. 8 min · 8 flashcards

The idea

Your Main Deck is the pile you shuffle and draw from. The Extra Deck is a second pile that sits next to it. You never draw from it. You never shuffle it into anything.

It holds your Synchro Monsters and your Fusion Monsters. They wait there until you have the right ingredients on the field.

Think of it like this

Your Main Deck is a bag of random tools. You reach in and grab whatever comes out. The Extra Deck is a toolbox on the wall. Every tool is in its place. When a job comes up, you take the exact tool you need.

The rules

  • Up to 15 cards. It does not count toward your 40-card Main Deck.
  • It is secret. You can look at your own. Your opponent cannot.
  • The banlist still counts. is Limited, so you can have only one in your Main, Extra, and Side Deck combined.

What happens when a Synchro leaves the field?

This is where most new players get confused. It depends on where the Synchro is going.

A Synchro that goes back to the Extra Deck can be summoned again later. That is why bouncing a Synchro is a good trade, but not a full answer.

Bringing a Synchro back from the Graveyard

Cards like can revive a Synchro from the Graveyard. But only if it was properly Synchro Summoned first. A Synchro that never landed on the field the right way cannot be revived.

The toolbox

Most Edison decks run almost the same Extra Deck: one copy of each strong, generic Synchro. Each one solves a different problem. You pick the one the moment calls for.

Common beginner mistakes
  • Putting a bounced Synchro in your hand. It goes back to the Extra Deck.
  • Counting the Extra Deck toward your 40 cards. It does not count.
  • Running 3 copies of one Synchro. One of each different answer is usually better.

Recap

  • The Extra Deck holds up to 15 Synchros and Fusions. You never draw from it.
  • Bounced or shuffled Synchros go back to the Extra Deck.
  • Destroyed Synchros go to the Graveyard. They can be revived only if they were properly summoned.
  • Most decks run a toolbox of one each of the generic Synchros.
