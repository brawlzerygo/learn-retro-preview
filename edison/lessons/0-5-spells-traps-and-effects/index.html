Spells, Traps, and Effects

Each kind of Spell and Trap, and the kinds of monster effects. 9 min · 6 flashcards

Already know how to play? This part is optional.Skip to Edison 101 →

Spells

You can play a Spell from your hand in your Main Phase. You can also Set it face-down to use later. The small icon on the card tells you its kind.

Spell typeDescriptionExample
NormalDoes its thing once, then goes to the Graveyard.
EquipAttaches to a monster. It stays while that monster stays face-up on the field.
ContinuousStays on the field and keeps working.
FieldGoes in the Field Zone and changes the whole field.
RitualUsed to summon a Ritual Monster. Rare in Edison.
Quick-PlayThe fast Spell. Play it in any phase of your turn, or Set it to use on your opponent's turn.

Traps

A Trap must be Set face-down first. You cannot activate it the turn you Set it. From the next turn on, you can activate it on either player's turn, as soon as what it needs happens.

Trap typeDescriptionExample
NormalDoes its thing once, then goes to the Graveyard.
ContinuousStays on the field and keeps working.
CounterThe fastest card in the game. Usually stops something.
Think of it like this

A Spell is a move you make. A Trap is a tripwire. You lay it down, wait, and it goes off when your opponent steps on it.

Monster effects

Orange-framed monsters have effects. They come in five kinds. You do not need to memorize the names yet, but they explain when an effect can happen.

Effect typeWhen it worksExample
ContinuousAlways on while the monster is face-up. Nothing to activate. cannot be destroyed in battle
IgnitionYou choose to use it in your Main Phase.
TriggerHappens when something specific happens., when it is sent from the field to the Graveyard
FlipHappens when the monster is flipped face-up.
QuickCan be used on either player's turn, like a Trap.

Costs and targets

Many cards say something like "Pay 800 LP" or "discard 1 card" before the main effect. That first part is the cost. You pay it when you activate the card. If the card is stopped, you still lost the cost.

A card targets when it picks a specific card, like ("change 1 face-up monster"). A card that hits everything, like Mirror Force, does not target.

Common beginner mistakes
  • Setting a Trap and using it the same turn.
  • Playing a Normal Spell on your opponent's turn. Only Quick-Play Spells can be used then, and only if they were Set before.
  • Expecting the cost back when your card gets stopped.

Recap

  • Spells: play them in your Main Phase. Quick-Plays are faster.
  • Traps: Set first, wait a turn, then use them on either turn.
  • Monster effects: Continuous, Ignition, Trigger, Flip, and Quick.
  • Costs are paid up front, even if the card gets stopped.
