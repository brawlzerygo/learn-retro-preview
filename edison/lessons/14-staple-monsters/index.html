Staple Monsters

The Main Deck monsters you will see in game after game. 4 min · 6 flashcards

Monsters that come out for free

These monsters reach the field without your Normal Summon. That leaves your Normal Summon for a Tuner.

Cyber Dragon

is in 76% of the decks on this site. If only your opponent controls a monster, you can Special Summon it from your hand. It is a Level 5 with 2100 ATK. It is Semi-Limited.

It is also the key to (89% of decks). Send Cyber Dragon plus any Machines from either field to the Graveyard. Its ATK is 1000 for each material. Catastor and Armory Arm are Machines, so your opponent's Synchros can become your material.

Gorz the Emissary of Darkness

(47%, Limited) waits in your hand. When you take damage from your opponent's card while you control no cards, you can Special Summon it.

  • Battle damage: you also get a token with ATK and DEF equal to the damage.
  • Effect damage: your opponent takes the same damage.

So an empty field is a risk for your opponent. A direct attack can give you a 2700 ATK monster and a token.

Tragoedia

(24% of decks, just under the staple line, Limited) also waits in your hand. When you take battle damage, you can Special Summon it. It gains 600 ATK and DEF for each card in your hand. Once per turn, it can also send a monster from your hand to the Graveyard to take control of your opponent's face-up monster with the same Level.

Two rulings
  • You can only activate one of these hand effects per chain. You cannot summon Gorz and Tragoedia off the same damage.
  • Tragoedia's ATK is "?" in the Deck, so cannot search it.

Monsters that trade

These monsters give you something when they go. You lose the monster, but you do not lose the card.

Sangan

(33%, Limited): when it goes from the field to the Graveyard, add a monster with 1500 or less ATK from your Deck to your hand. In Edison, you can use that monster's effects the same turn. Sangan is also a Level 3, so it makes good Synchro Material: you get a Synchro and a search.

Ryko, Lightsworn Hunter

(35%) is a Flip monster. When flipped, you can destroy one card on the field. Then you always send the top 3 cards of your Deck to the Graveyard. Destroying is optional; the 3 cards always go.

Caius the Shadow Monarch

(40%) is a Level 6, so it needs one Tribute. When Tribute Summoned, it banishes one card on the field. If that card was a DARK monster, its controller also takes 1000 damage. Banishing gets past Stardust Dragon, which only stops destruction.

Cards that stop Tributes, like Mask of Restrict, stop Caius. So does Pulling the Rug (Lesson 16).

Monsters that stop plays

D.D. Crow

(64%) works from your hand at any time. Discard it to banish one card from your opponent's Graveyard. Use it on:

  • A that wants to come back.
  • The target of Call of the Haunted or Pot of Avarice.
  • A DARK monster, when your opponent needs DARKs in the Graveyard.

Fossil Dyna Pachycephalo

(36%) is a Flip monster. When flipped face-up, it destroys every Special Summoned monster on the field. While it stays face-up, neither player can Special Summon. That means no Synchros for either side.

The big idea

Fossil Dyna turns off Synchros entirely. If your deck relies on Normal Summons and your opponent relies on Synchros, it hurts them more than you.

Common beginner mistakes
  • Activating Gorz while you still control a card. You must control none.
  • Leaving Plaguespreader in your Graveyard when your opponent might hold D.D. Crow.
  • Trying to Synchro Summon while Fossil Dyna is face-up. Nobody can Special Summon.

Recap

  • Cyber Dragon, Gorz and Tragoedia come out without your Normal Summon.
  • Chimeratech Fortress Dragon uses Machines from either field.
  • Sangan, Ryko and Caius trade one card for value.
  • D.D. Crow attacks the Graveyard. Fossil Dyna stops all Special Summons.
