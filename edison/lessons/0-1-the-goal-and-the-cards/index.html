The Goal and the Cards

How you win, what you start with, and how to read a card. 6 min · 6 flashcards

Already know how to play? This part is optional.Skip to Edison 101 →

The goal

Yu-Gi-Oh! is a game for two players. Each player has a Deck of cards. You take turns playing cards to attack your opponent and to stop their plans.

Each player starts with 8000 Life Points (LP). You win when:

  • Your opponent's LP hits 0, or
  • Your opponent has to draw a card but their Deck is empty, or
  • A card says you win. The famous one is Exodia: hold all 5 pieces in your hand.

Almost every game is won the first way. You lower your opponent's LP with attacks and effects until it reaches 0.

What you start with

DeckSizeDescription
Main Deck40 to 60 cardsThe pile you draw from. Most players use exactly 40, so they draw their best cards more often.
Extra Deck0 to 15 cardsSpecial monsters that wait on the side. You never draw from it. Edison 101 covers it in Lesson 5.
Side Deck0 to 15 cardsSpare cards you can swap in between games of a match.

At the start, both players shuffle and draw 5 cards. That is your opening hand.

You can have up to 3 copies of any card, unless the banlist says fewer. Lesson 8 covers the banlist.

The three kinds of cards

You can tell them apart by the color of the frame.

Card typeFrame colorDescriptionExample
MonsterYellow or orangeAttacks and defends on the field. Orange ones have an effect.
SpellGreenA one-time trick or a lasting bonus. Usually played on your own turn.
TrapPinkA surprise. Set it face-down first, then spring it later, often on your opponent's turn.
Think of it like this

Monsters are your soldiers. Spells are the orders you give on your turn. Traps are the ambush you set up and wait to spring.

How to read a monster

Tap above to see it big. Every monster has the same parts:

  • Name at the top.
  • Attribute, the symbol in the top right: DARK, LIGHT, EARTH, WATER, FIRE, or WIND.
  • Level, the stars under the name. Gene-Warped Warwolf has 4. The Level decides how hard a monster is to summon.
  • Type, in brackets above the text, like [Beast-Warrior] or [Fiend / Effect].
  • Text: either a bit of story (no effect) or its effect.
  • ATK (attack) and DEF (defense) at the bottom.
Old card words

Cards from 2010 often say "remove from play." Newer cards say "banish." They mean the same thing: the card goes to a pile set apart from the Graveyard, and it is very hard to get back.

Recap

  • Both players start with 8000 LP and 5 cards. Bring your opponent to 0.
  • The Main Deck is 40 to 60 cards. You may run up to 3 copies of a card.
  • Monsters fight, Spells are tricks you play on your turn, Traps are set and sprung later.
  • A monster has a Level, Attribute, Type, ATK, and DEF.
