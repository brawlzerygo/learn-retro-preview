The deck

Monarchs are Level 6 monsters with an effect when they are Tribute Summoned. Each needs one Tribute. Frogs supply those Tributes, turn after turn. The Junk Frog list and the Hero Frog list are both listed in the Best tier on this site.

Junk Frog also runs Unifrog, Tragoedia, Gorz, Dark Armed Dragon, 2 Gold Sarcophagus, Scapegoat, Cold Wave, 2 Threatening Roar and only one Mirror Force.

Treeborn Frog: the free Tribute

is the heart of the deck. Tribute it for a Monarch, and it waits in the GY. Next Standby Phase, if you control no Spells or Traps, it Special Summons itself. In Edison this effect is not once per turn.

Think of it like this

Treeborn is a boomerang. You throw it away to pay for a Monarch, and it flies back on your next turn, ready to be thrown again.

That explains the deck list. Junk Frog has 12 Spells and only 3 Traps. Most Spells are used right away and leave the field. A Trap sits set on the field, and a set card stops Treeborn from coming back.

The Monarch loop
  1. Standby Phase: Treeborn returns from the GY.
  2. Main Phase: Tribute Treeborn to Tribute Summon a Monarch. Its effect removes one of your opponent's cards.
  3. Next turn, do it again. Each loop is a free card.

The Frog engine and Junk Synchron

The other Frogs move cards around to set up Treeborn and Synchros.

  • can be Special Summoned by discarding another WATER, like Treeborn. When it is summoned, it can send a Level 2 or lower WATER Aqua from the Deck to the GY, like Treeborn or Dupe Frog. It can also return a monster you control to your hand to let you Normal Summon one more Frog that turn.
  • Tributes a monster to Special Summon any Frog from the Deck.
  • adds a Frog from the Deck or GY when it goes from the field to the GY. Ruling: if Dupe is Tributed for Substitoad's cost, it misses the timing and adds nothing.

Here is a short turn from the cards' text. Your hand has Swap Frog, Treeborn Frog and Junk Synchron.

  1. Discard Treeborn Frog to Special Summon Swap Frog. Treeborn is now in the GY, ready to return.
  2. Swap Frog's summon effect sends Dupe Frog from the Deck to the GY.
  3. Normal Summon . Revive Treeborn (Level 1) in Defense Position with its effects negated.
  4. Junk (3) + Swap (2) + Treeborn (1) = Level 6: Goyo Guardian or Brionac. Treeborn goes back to the GY and can return next Standby.

Junk Synchron also makes Junk Warrior (Level 5, with any Level 2 Frog), which gains the ATK of all your Level 2 and lower monsters. Junk (3) + Treeborn (1) makes Armory Arm (Level 4).

Defense and the Hero Frog version

Frog Monarchs defend without Traps. Dupe Frog forces attacks into itself, and Substitoad keeps Frogs from being destroyed by battle. Battle Fader stops a direct attack and ends the Battle Phase. Tragoedia comes out from the hand when you take battle damage. Threatening Roar stops attacks for a turn.

Hero Frog keeps the Frogs and Monarchs, and adds a HERO package from Lesson 23. Frogs are WATER, so Miracle Fusion can banish a HERO and a Frog from the GY to make Absolute Zero. It runs 3 copies of it. Evil HERO Infernal Prodigy draws a card at the End Phase if it was Tributed for a HERO Tribute Summon, like Evil HERO Malicious Edge. It also plays 3 Raigeki Break and Soul Exchange.

Weaknesses and how to play against it

  • Treeborn in the GY. D.D. Crow or Soul Release banishes it. The loop stops.
  • Special Summons. Royal Oppression, Fossil Dyna Pachycephalo and Vanity's Fiend stop Treeborn, Swap Frog and Substitoad. Monarchs are Tribute Summoned, so these do not stop them.
  • Summon triggers. A Tribute Summon is a kind of Normal Summon, so Pulling the Rug can negate a Monarch's effect. It also stops Junk Synchron. Solemn Judgment and Bottomless Trap Hole hit Monarchs as they land.
  • The battle wall. Remove Substitoad or Dupe Frog with an effect before you attack.
Common mistakes against Frogs
  • Attacking into Dupe Frog with Substitoad on the field. Frogs can't be destroyed by battle.
  • Leaving Treeborn in the GY when you hold D.D. Crow. Banish it before their Standby Phase.
  • Attacking directly into an empty field with no plan for Battle Fader or Tragoedia.

IMNerd (Frazier Smith) teaches this deck in his "Frog School" series in the Library.

Recap

  • Treeborn Frog returns each Standby Phase if you control no Spells or Traps, giving free Tributes.
  • Caius, Raiza and Mobius each remove a card when Tribute Summoned.
  • Swap Frog, Substitoad, Dupe Frog and Junk Synchron set up the GY and make Synchros.
  • Banish Treeborn, lock Special Summons and use Pulling the Rug to beat it.
