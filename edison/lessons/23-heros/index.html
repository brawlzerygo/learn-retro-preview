Four decks, one toolkit

This site lists four HERO decks. They share a core of searchers and fusions, then go different ways.

Miracle Fusion is the glue. It takes materials from the GY, so a HERO and a WATER monster that died earlier become Absolute Zero later. Ocean is both a HERO and a WATER, so Ocean alone in the GY with any other HERO is enough.

Absolute Zero is a trap for removal

destroys all your monsters when it leaves the field. That includes being destroyed, bounced, banished or returned to the Extra Deck. The ruling exception: if it is face-down when it leaves, nothing happens. Book of Moon it first, then remove it.

Diva Hero

is a Level 2 Tuner. When Normal Summoned, it Special Summons a Level 3 or lower Sea Serpent from the Deck: Spined Gillman (Level 3) or another Diva. is a Level 6 DARK. From the GY, it banishes itself to Special Summon another Malicious from the Deck.

  1. Discard Malicious to Special Summon Dark Grepher. Then banish that Malicious from the GY to Special Summon a second Malicious from the Deck.
  2. Normal Summon Deep Sea Diva and Special Summon Spined Gillman.
  3. Diva (2) + Malicious (6) = Level 8, like Stardust Dragon. Or Diva (2) + Gillman (3) = Level 5, like Ally of Justice Catastor.

The list adds Plaguespreader Zombie (another Level 2 Tuner), 3 Caius, Dark Armed Dragon and Future Fusion. Future Fusion reveals a Fusion and sends its materials from your Deck to the GY, like a Malicious and a WATER monster for Absolute Zero. Those cards are then ready for Malicious's effect and Miracle Fusion.

Hero Beat and Diva Hero Beat

is a Gemini monster. It is a Normal Monster on the field and in the GY until you Normal Summon it a second time. Hero Beat uses that:

  • Gemini Spark Tributes a face-up Level 4 Gemini to destroy a card and draw 1.
  • returns an Elemental HERO Normal Monster from the GY, like Alius, to your hand. Then it destroys an opponent's monster with ATK equal to or lower than it, so up to 1900.
  • Honest gives a battling LIGHT monster the ATK of its opponent. Alius, Captain Gold and Thunder King are LIGHT.

Hero Beat is a stun deck. It runs 2 , 2 Royal Oppression and Thunder King Rai-Oh. Skill Drain negates the effects of face-up monsters. Alius is a plain Normal Monster on the field anyway, so it barely cares. Thunder King stops searching except by drawing. Captain Gold adds Skyscraper, which gives an Elemental HERO 1000 ATK when it attacks a bigger monster. Its Extra Deck has three each of Absolute Zero, Gaia, Superalloy Beast Raptinus, Chimeratech Fortress Dragon and Stardust Dragon. Stardust can arrive through Starlight Road.

Diva Hero Beat mixes both ideas. It runs 3 Diva, 2 Gillman, 3 Alius, 3 Gemini Spark and 14 Traps. Its Extra Deck adds Sea Dragon Lord Gishilnodon, which needs a Tuner plus exactly one Level 3 non-Tuner. Diva and Gillman fit that.

Weaknesses and how to play against them

  • Searching. Thunder King Rai-Oh stops E - Emergency Call, Stratos's search and Reinforcement of the Army.
  • Banishing from the GY. Kycoo the Ghost Destroyer stops Miracle Fusion's GY materials and Malicious's effect.
  • Normal Summon triggers. Pulling the Rug negates Deep Sea Diva and a Normal Summoned Stratos.
  • Diva Hero is mostly DARK. Malicious, Caius, Dark Grepher and Dark Armed Dragon all fall to Consecrated Light.
  • Hero Beat locks you. Save Mystical Space Typhoon or Dust Tornado for Skill Drain or Royal Oppression.
Common mistakes against HEROs
  • Destroying a face-up Absolute Zero while you have monsters out. Flip it face-down first, or clear your own board.
  • Ignoring their GY. Miracle Fusion can use it at any time.
  • Attacking a LIGHT monster when they hold a card. Honest can match your ATK.

Recap

  • All HERO decks share E - Emergency Call, Stratos, Miracle Fusion and Absolute Zero.
  • Diva Hero uses Diva and Malicious for quick Level 5 and Level 8 Synchros.
  • Hero Beat uses Gemini beaters behind Skill Drain and Royal Oppression.
  • Flip Absolute Zero face-down before you remove it.
