The deck

Vayu Turbo uses only a few Blackwings. It does not search with Black Whirlwind. It sends Vayu and Sirocco straight to the Graveyard and summons Synchros from there. "Turbo" means it races to set this up. The Vayu Turbo list is listed in the Best tier on this site.

The engine

Vayu needs two things in the GY: itself and a non-Tuner Blackwing. In this deck, that Blackwing is Sirocco.

Here is one turn that follows the cards' text. Your hand has Armageddon Knight, Dark Grepher and Sirocco.

  1. Normal Summon . Send Vayu from your Deck to the GY.
  2. Discard Sirocco to Special Summon . Sirocco and Vayu are both in the GY.
  3. Use Vayu: banish Vayu and Sirocco and Special Summon (1 + 5 = 6) from the Extra Deck. Its effects are negated, but it still has 2300 ATK.
  4. You control three monsters: 1400, 1700 and 2300 ATK. That is a lot of damage from three cards.

What comes after Armed Wing

Armed Wing is a Level 6 non-Tuner on the field. That opens more doors:

  • (2) + Armed Wing (6) = Level 8: Stardust Dragon, Colossal Fighter, Thought Ruler Archfiend or Dark End Dragon. Dark End needs DARK non-Tuners, and Armed Wing is DARK.
  • Armed Wing is a Blackwing, so Gale can Special Summon itself next to it. Gale (3) + Dark Grepher (4) = Level 7, like Blackwing Armor Master.
  • Armed Wing can be a Tribute for Caius.
Vayu rulings to know
  • The Synchro is not Synchro Summoned. It can't be revived later by effects like Goyo Guardian.
  • If Royal Oppression is chained to Vayu's effect, Vayu is not banished. It can try again later that turn.
  • If the Synchro can't be summoned when the effect resolves, Vayu and the target are still banished.
Exactly 3 DARKs

needs exactly 3 DARK monsters in the GY. A deck that sends many DARKs there can go past 3. Vayu banishing two DARKs helps bring the count back down.

Weaknesses and how to play against it

  • It lives in the Graveyard. D.D. Crow can banish Vayu or Sirocco from the GY before they are used. Soul Release banishes up to 5 cards. Kycoo the Ghost Destroyer stops your opponent from banishing from either GY, which turns off Vayu and Dark Armed Dragon.
  • It Special Summons. , Fossil Dyna Pachycephalo and Vanity's Fiend stop Vayu, Dark Grepher and Plaguespreader.
  • Almost all DARK. Consecrated Light stops DARKs from being summoned or attacking.
  • Normal Summon triggers. Pulling the Rug negates Armageddon Knight's effect when it is Normal Summoned.
Common mistakes against Vayu Turbo
  • Watching only the field. Count Vayus and Siroccos in their GY every turn.
  • Using D.D. Crow on a random card. Save it for Vayu or Sirocco.
  • Forgetting Burial. Banished Vayus can come back to the GY at Quick-Play speed.

Recap

  • Vayu Turbo sends Vayu and Sirocco to the GY with Armageddon Knight, Dark Grepher and mills.
  • Vayu + Sirocco makes a Level 6 Armed Wing with its effects negated.
  • Armed Wing then helps make Level 7 and 8 Synchros, or feeds Caius.
  • GY removal, Special Summon locks and Consecrated Light slow it down.
