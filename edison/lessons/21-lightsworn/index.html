Lightsworn

A deck that throws its own cards into the Graveyard, on purpose. 4 min · 6 flashcards

At a glance: Lightsworn

The deck

Lightsworn monsters are LIGHT. Most of them send cards from the top of your Deck to the GY at the end of your turn. Players call that "milling." The Lightsworn list is listed as a Competitive deck on this site.

Think of it like this

Most decks treat the Graveyard as a trash can. Lightsworn treats it as a second hand. Every card that falls in is a resource.

The plan

Lightsworn plays a steady game. Early on, it summons a Lightsworn, mills at the End Phase, and uses Solar Recharge and Charge to draw and dig. Wulf and Necro Gardna make the mill pay off. Once four different Lightsworn names are in the GY, Judgment Dragon can come out from the hand. It can pay 1000 LP to destroy every other card on the field, and then attack with 3000 ATK.

The cost of the mill

A 40-card Deck shrinks fast. Lyla and Judgment Dragon alone send 7 cards a turn. Long games can end with a Lightsworn player running out of cards, so they need to win before that.

Weaknesses and how to play against it

  • Light-Imprisoning Mirror negates LIGHT monster effects activated on the field or in the GY. That covers the End Phase mills, Wulf and Judgment Dragon's effect.
  • Graveyard removal. D.D. Crow and Soul Release banish cards from the GY. Fewer Lightsworn names there means no Judgment Dragon.
  • Special Summon locks. , Fossil Dyna Pachycephalo and Vanity's Fiend stop Wulf, Lumina's revive and Judgment Dragon.
  • Big summons. Bottomless Trap Hole hits Judgment Dragon, Celestia and Wulf.
Common mistakes against Lightsworn
  • Putting your whole hand on the field. Judgment Dragon can clear it all. Keep cards back.
  • Forgetting Necro Gardna in their GY. Plan for one attack to be negated.
  • Counting their GY loosely. Count the different Lightsworn names. Three means one more mill could bring Judgment Dragon.

Recap

  • Lightsworn mills itself at the End Phase and with Ryko, Celestia, Charge and Solar Recharge.
  • Wulf, Necro Gardna and Plaguespreader turn milled cards into value.
  • Four different Lightsworn names in the GY unlock Judgment Dragon.
  • Light-Imprisoning Mirror, GY removal and Special Summon locks hurt it most.
