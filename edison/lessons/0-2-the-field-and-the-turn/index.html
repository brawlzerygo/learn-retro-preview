The Field and the Turn

Where cards go, and the six phases of every turn. 7 min · 6 flashcards

Already know how to play? This part is optional.Skip to Edison 101 →

The field

Each player has their own side of the table. It has these spots:

ZoneNumberDescription
Monster Zones5Your monsters, face-up or face-down
Spell & Trap Zones5Your Spells and Traps, face-up or set face-down
Field Zone1A Field Spell. In 2010 there is only one for the whole table (Lesson 10).
Deck1 pileFace-down. You draw from the top.
Graveyard (GY)1 pileFace-up. Used and destroyed cards go here. Either player can look through it.
Extra Deck1 pileFace-down. Only you can look at it.

Banished cards sit face-up in their own pile, away from the Graveyard.

Think of it like this

The Graveyard is a discard pile, but a busy one. Many cards can pull things back out of it. A banished card is gone from the game, almost for good.

The six phases

Every turn goes through the same phases, in the same order.

  1. Draw Phase. Draw 1 card.
  2. Standby Phase. Usually nothing happens. Some cards say "during your Standby Phase" and act here.
  3. Main Phase 1. Most of your turn. Summon monsters, play Spells, set Traps, use effects.
  4. Battle Phase. Attack with your monsters. Optional: you can skip it.
  5. Main Phase 2. Like Main Phase 1. Use it for anything you want to do after your attacks.
  6. End Phase. "End of turn" effects happen. If you hold more than 6 cards, discard down to 6.

Then your opponent takes a turn, through the same six phases.

The first turn

The player who goes first cannot attack on their first turn. They skip the Battle Phase. In Edison, they still draw a card in their Draw Phase (modern rules skip that draw).

What you can do in Main Phase

  • Normal Summon or Set one monster (Lesson 0.3)
  • Special Summon as many monsters as your cards allow
  • Play Spells and set Spells and Traps, as many as you have zones for
  • Change a monster's position, once per monster
  • Use monster effects
Common beginner mistakes
  • Attacking on the very first turn of the game. The first player cannot.
  • Forgetting Main Phase 2. A monster you summon after battle is safer, because your opponent has seen your attacks.
  • Ending your turn with 7 cards. Discard down to 6.

Recap

  • 5 Monster Zones, 5 Spell & Trap Zones, a Field Zone, a Deck, a Graveyard, and an Extra Deck.
  • Draw, Standby, Main 1, Battle, Main 2, End.
  • The first player cannot attack on turn 1.
  • Discard down to 6 in your End Phase.
