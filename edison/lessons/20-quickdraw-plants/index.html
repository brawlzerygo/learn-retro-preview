The deck

You met this engine in Lesson 6. According to edisonformat.com, a Quickdraw deck called Quickdraw Dandywarrior won the April 2010 tournament in Edison that the format is named after. On this site the Quickdraw Plant list is listed as a Rogue deck.

The rules of the two Tuners

Both Tuners come with limits written on the card. The whole deck is built around them.

  • can only be Synchro Material for a monster that lists a "Synchron" Tuner. In this Extra Deck that means , Turbo Warrior, and Nitro Warrior.
  • can only be material for a Dragon Synchro, and the other materials can't be Level 4. That means Black Rose Dragon, , Iron Chain Dragon, Stardust Dragon and Red Dragon Archfiend.

A sample turn

Your hand has Quickdraw Synchron, Dandylion and Debris Dragon.

  1. Use Quickdraw's effect: send Dandylion from your hand to the GY and Special Summon Quickdraw. Dandylion makes 2 Fluff Tokens.
  2. Synchro Summon Drill Warrior with Quickdraw (5) and 1 Fluff (1).
  3. Normal Summon Debris Dragon. Revive Dandylion (300 ATK) in Attack Position with its effects negated.
  4. Synchro Summon Ancient Fairy Dragon with Debris (4) and Dandylion (3). Dandylion is in the GY again, off the field, so it makes 2 more Fluff Tokens.
  5. You end with Drill Warrior, Ancient Fairy Dragon and 3 Fluff Tokens from three cards.
Black Rose and Dandylion

If you Synchro Summon Black Rose Dragon using Dandylion, Black Rose's "destroy all cards" can only be Chain Link 2. Dandylion's tokens are summoned after everything is destroyed, so they survive.

The Volcanic version

The Quickdraw Volcanic list (listed as a Contender deck) swaps the Plants for Pyros. Quickdraw sends from the hand to the GY. Shell in the GY can pay 500 LP to add another Shell from the Deck. sends a Pyro with 500 or less ATK from your hand, like Shell, to destroy a monster. Volcanic Rocket adds Blaze Accelerator when it is Summoned. The list also runs 2 Royal Decree, which negates all other Trap effects on the field.

Weaknesses and how to play against it

  • Special Summons everywhere. can negate Quickdraw's effect, the tokens and the Synchro. Fossil Dyna Pachycephalo and Vanity's Fiend stop Special Summons outright.
  • Debris needs a Normal Summon. Pulling the Rug negates its revive and destroys it.
  • Synchros can be stopped as they land. Thunder King Rai-Oh can negate a Synchro Summon. Solemn Judgment can too.
  • Tytannial only stops cards that target. Use cards that don't target, like Black Rose Dragon or Heavy Storm, against a Tytannial board.
Common mistakes against Quickdraw
  • Destroying Dandylion. Destroyed or sent anywhere to the GY, it still makes tokens.
  • Thinking Quickdraw can make Stardust Dragon. It can't. Stardust needs a generic Tuner.
  • Ignoring the Fluff Tokens. Each one is a Level for the next Synchro.

E3 Yu-Gi-Oh has a full Quickdraw Dandywarrior guide in the Library.

Recap

  • Quickdraw makes only "Synchron" Synchros. Debris makes only Dragon Synchros.
  • Dandylion makes 2 tokens every time it reaches the GY, from anywhere.
  • Lonefire and Tytannial use Plants, and Fluff Tokens are Plants.
  • Special Summon locks, Pulling the Rug and summon negation beat it.
