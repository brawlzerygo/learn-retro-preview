Summoning Monsters

Normal Summon, Set, Tribute, Flip, and Special Summon. 8 min · 7 flashcards

Already know how to play? This part is optional.Skip to Edison 101 →

The Normal Summon

Once per turn, in your Main Phase, you can put one monster from your hand onto the field. That is your Normal Summon.

You choose how it goes down:

  • Summon it face-up in Attack Position (card upright). It can attack.
  • Set it face-down in Defense Position (card sideways). Your opponent cannot see it. Setting a monster uses your Normal Summon too.
The rule

One Normal Summon or Set per turn. That is it.

Tribute Summons: bigger monsters cost more

A monster's Level decides what it costs to Normal Summon.

LevelTributes neededExample
1 to 4None (Level 4)
5 or 61 monster (Level 6)
7 or higher2 monsters (Level 7)

To Tribute, you send monsters you control to the Graveyard. A Tribute Summon is your Normal Summon for the turn.

Think of it like this

A big monster is an expensive purchase. You pay for it with the monsters you already have on the field.

Positions

  • Attack Position: face-up, upright. It can attack. If it is attacked, its ATK is used.
  • Defense Position: sideways. It cannot attack. If it is attacked, its DEF is used. It can be face-up or face-down.

Once per turn, you can switch a monster's position. You cannot switch it the turn it was summoned, or after it attacked that turn.

Flip Summons

A Set (face-down) monster can be turned face-up into Attack Position. That is a Flip Summon. You cannot do it the turn you Set the monster. It does not use your Normal Summon.

Some monsters have FLIP effects, like . They work when the monster is flipped face-up, either by a Flip Summon or by being attacked while face-down.

Special Summons

A Special Summon is any summon that a card makes happen. There is no limit on how many you can do in a turn.

  • Some monsters summon themselves. can be Special Summoned from your hand if your opponent controls a monster and you control none.
  • Some cards bring monsters back from the Graveyard, or pull them from the Deck.

Edison's big new summon, the Synchro Summon, is a Special Summon. That is why it does not use your Normal Summon. Lesson 4 shows how it works.

Common beginner mistakes
  • Normal Summoning, then also Setting a monster. That is two. You only get one.
  • Trying to Tribute Summon a Level 7 with one Tribute. It needs two.
  • Flip Summoning the turn you Set the monster.

Recap

  • One Normal Summon or Set per turn.
  • Level 5 and 6 need 1 Tribute. Level 7 and up need 2.
  • Attack Position uses ATK. Defense Position uses DEF and cannot attack.
  • Special Summons have no limit.
