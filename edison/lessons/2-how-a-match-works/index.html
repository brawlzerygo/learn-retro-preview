How a Match Works

Best of three, who goes first, and the three piles of cards you bring. 6 min · 7 flashcards

A match is best 2 of 3

At a tournament, or in a serious game online, you don't play just one duel. You play a match: up to three duels against the same opponent. The first player to win 2 duels wins the match.

Think of it like this

A match is like a short series in sports. One lucky game does not decide it. You have to win twice.

Who goes first

  1. Duel 1: play rock-paper-scissors or flip a coin. The winner picks whether to go first or second.
  2. Duel 2 and 3: the player who lost the last duel picks.

Both players start with 8000 Life Points and draw 5 cards.

The first turn in Edison
  • The player who goes first still draws in their first Draw Phase, so they start their turn with 6 cards.
  • The player who goes first cannot attack on that turn. There is no Battle Phase on turn 1.

Going first lets you set up your field and your Set cards first. Going second means you are the first player who can attack.

The three piles you bring

DeckSizeDescription
Main Deck40 to 60The cards you shuffle and draw from. Almost everyone plays exactly 40, so they draw their best cards more often.
Extra Deck0 to 15Synchro and Fusion Monsters only. You never draw from it. Lesson 5 covers it.
Side Deck0 to 15Extra cards you can swap in between the duels of a match.

On this site, 49 of the 55 deck lists run exactly 40 cards in the Main Deck, and all 55 bring a full Side Deck of 15.

Three copies, across all three piles

You can run up to 3 copies of a card. That count covers your Main, Extra and Side Deck together. The banlist can lower it: is Limited, so you can have only 1, in any one of the three piles. Lesson 8 covers the banlist.

Siding: changing your deck between duels

After duel 1, you know what your opponent plays. Your Side Deck lets you adjust. You swap cards card for card: take one card out of your Main or Extra Deck and put one Side Deck card in its place.

  • Every pile keeps its size. If your Main Deck was 40, it is still 40.
  • Show your opponent how many cards are in your Side Deck before the match, and again after each swap. That proves you swapped one for one.
  • You only side between duels, never during one.

Common beginner mistakes
  • Skipping the first draw. In Edison, the first player draws.
  • Trying to attack on turn 1 when you went first.
  • Forgetting that a Limited card counts across all three piles, Side Deck included.
  • Siding in a card without taking one out.

Recap

  • A match is best 2 of 3 duels.
  • Duel 1: rock-paper-scissors or a coin flip decides who picks. After that, the loser of the last duel picks.
  • Going first, you draw (6 cards) but you cannot attack.
  • Main Deck 40 to 60 (almost always 40), Extra Deck up to 15, Side Deck up to 15.
  • 3 copies at most across all three piles, unless the banlist says fewer.
  • Side card for card, and show your Side Deck count.

These rules are on the Rules page, from the Official Rulebook, version 7.0.
