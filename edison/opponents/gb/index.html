Gladiator Beasts

Control · taught in Lesson 25

✕

Control · Lists: Gladiator Beast · Lesson 25

How to spot it

A Gladiator Beast attacks, then shuffles itself into the Deck at the end of the Battle Phase and a different one comes out with a bonus effect.

What it wants

Win a battle, tag out for a fresh Gladiator Beast with a bonus, and build to or .

Strengths

  • Each tag out is a free monster with a free effect.
  • negates a monster effect while they control a Gladiator Beast.
  • Shrink, Book of Moon and Dimensional Prison protect its battles.

Weaknesses

  • It needs to battle: banishes the attacker before damage, so it never tags out.
  • The whole engine is Special Summons: and stop it.
  • makes a monster forget it was summoned by a Gladiator Beast.

What to expect

  • swapping a Gladiator Beast without a battle.
  • destroying a Spell or Trap, a monster.
  • Heraklinos negating any Spell or Trap for a discard.

How to beat it

  • Make it attack into your Traps instead of attacking into it.
  • Hit a Normal Summoned Gladiator Beast before it battles: it has no bonus.
  • Use Spells and Traps against War Chariot, since it only negates monster effects.

Don't

  • Attacking into a Gladiator Beast with a monster it can beat: every win is a tag out.
  • Using a monster effect while War Chariot is Set.
