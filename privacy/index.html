Privacy

Learn Retro is a free, fan-made site. There are no accounts, no ads and nothing to buy.

Google Analytics

If you click Accept on the cookie banner, the site loads Google Analytics, which sets cookies to count visits: which pages are read, roughly where visitors come from, and what device they use. We use it only to see which lessons and tools people use, so we know what to build next. Google processes this data under its own privacy policy. If you click Decline, Google Analytics never loads and nothing is counted.

Saved in your browser

The site remembers some things in your own browser only, like your cookie choice, your theme, lesson progress and deck lists. They never leave your device, and clearing your browser data removes them.
